## Why this is only Candidate A

The retained 75 are the strongest *existing* source statements for a shorter version, but the old bank has a structural weakness: almost every statement is keyed in the socially favourable direction.

Therefore the likely V1 should **not** simply publish these 75 unchanged.

Next design pass should test replacing some retained/omitted statements with naturally written low-pole or trade-off items. The goal is not awkward negative wording; the goal is to reduce the possibility that a respondent can obtain a uniformly strong profile by repeatedly choosing the socially desirable answer.

## Dimension-level audit notes

### Kryptingumas
Main risk in source bank: overlap with Lankstumas and Ambicija; one item relies on colleagues' description.
Candidate A keeps the result-focused core and drops the clearest other-person-perception item.

### Struktūra
Main risk: several items are context-specific (documents, tidy workspace) or measure emotional comfort rather than structure itself.
Candidate A focuses on planning, system, time, order and accuracy.

### Bendradarbiavimas
Main risk: strong social desirability and overlap with positive team climate.
Candidate A prefers observable cooperative behaviours over broad beliefs such as “together is better”.

### Jautrumas
Main risk: some items claim unusually confident mind-reading (e.g. knowing hidden emotions) or drift into general helpfulness.
Candidate A focuses on noticing, considering and responding to emotional/social signals.

### Nuolankumas
Main risk: low ego can be confused with self-effacement, passivity or preference for invisibility.
Candidate A avoids making “working unnoticed” or undervaluing one's own contribution the ideal expression of humility.

### Konceptualus mąstymas
Main risk: self-claimed cognitive ability (“I understand complex concepts”) and overlap with creativity/learning.
Candidate A favours thinking habits: alternative angles, strategy, scenarios, pattern connections and future possibilities.

### Lankstumas
Main risk: overlap with learning, positive attitude to change and leadership style.
Candidate A focuses on behavioural adaptation rather than whether change feels exciting.

### Tobulėjimas
Main risk: specific learning channels (books, seminars) can favour role, age or profession rather than development orientation.
Candidate A focuses on learning intent and feedback behaviour independent of format.

### Ramybė
Main risk: emotional suppression is not the same construct as calm regulation; accepting criticism also overlaps with Nuolankumas.
Candidate A avoids defining calmness as “not showing emotion”.

### Pozityvumas
Main risk: several source items ask the respondent to infer how much better other people feel because of them.
Candidate A focuses more on the respondent's own orientation and behaviour.

### Savimonė
Main risk: overlap with Tobulėjimas through feedback seeking; some items claim certainty about others' impressions.
Candidate A keeps self-observation, reflection and awareness of impact.

### Ambicija
Main risk: ambition is mixed with status-seeking, competition and career-ladder orientation.
Candidate A focuses more on stretch, scale and meaningful achievement than on beating others or gaining status.

### Įtaka
Main risk: several items are leadership labels (“charismatic”, “natural leader”) or assume formal management.
Candidate A focuses on observable interpersonal influence that can exist without a manager title.

### Asertiškumas
Main risk: confidence, candour, negotiation and confrontation all sit nearby but are not identical.
Candidate A keeps direct expression, needs, refusal, conflict tolerance and boundaries as the clearest common core.

### Gyvybingumas
Main risk: several items depend on claims about how others perceive the respondent; initiative also overlaps with Ambicija/E-type behaviour.
Candidate A focuses on visible energy and participation.

## Exploratory legacy-data note

The 8 complete historical responses must **not** be used to validate the model. They are useful only as clues.

A first exploratory check shows:
- several dimensions move very strongly together in this tiny sample;
- Collaboration / Positivity / Vitality and Ambition / Influence / Vitality show especially strong co-movement;
- this may be real respondent pattern, sample artefact, or a common favourable-self-description factor;
- the current all-positive item wording makes the latter possibility important enough to test deliberately in the new beta.

No dimension or item should be removed solely because of these 8 responses.
