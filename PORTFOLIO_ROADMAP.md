## P1 — WorkStyle 15

### Historical source recovered

Uploaded `ADEPT15.xlsx` preserves the old Microsoft Forms implementation:
- 150 Likert statements scored 1–5;
- 15 dimensions × 10 statements;
- explicit statement-to-dimension map;
- 6 higher-level styles;
- raw Forms responses and Excel scoring logic.

Recovered legacy scoring:
- dimension range 10–50;
- normalized dimension index `(sum - 10) / 40 * 100`;
- style scores derived from grouped dimensions.

The workbook remains a historical/research source only. Raw respondent data must not be published to the public repo.

### Provenance finding

External review showed that the legacy **15 dimensions + 6 styles align essentially one-to-one with Aon's ADEPT-15 architecture**.

BPS describes ADEPT-15 as 15 traits grouped into 6 higher-order styles and states that 10 map to the Five Factor Model while 5 cover additional workplace/leadership-related traits.

The project owner does not recall the exact origin of the 150 Lithuanian statements and suspects they may have been generated by Microsoft Copilot from an international example. Treat this as plausible but unverified.

Important boundary:
- the legacy Forms tool is not the official ADEPT-15 instrument;
- do not use ADEPT-15 branding, proprietary item claims, adaptive scoring claims or imply equivalence to Aon's assessment.

### Architecture decision — independent WorkStyle 15

Chosen direction: **re-derive WorkStyle 15 as an independent workplace-reflection model** rather than retain the inherited 6 ADEPT-style groups.

New architecture: **5 domains × 3 dimensions**.

1. **Execution**
   - Goal Persistence / Kryptingumas
   - Work Structure / Struktūra
   - Achievement Drive / Pasiekimų siekis

2. **Adaptation**
   - Systems Thinking / Sisteminis mąstymas
   - Adaptability / Prisitaikymas
   - Learning Orientation / Mokymosi orientacija

3. **Collaboration**
   - Cooperation / Bendradarbiavimas
   - Empathic Attention / Empatinis dėmesys
   - Intellectual Humility / Intelektinis nuolankumas

4. **Influence**
   - Assertive Communication / Asertyvus komunikavimas
   - Persuasive Influence / Įtaka
   - Social Energy / Socialinė energija

5. **Self-Regulation**
   - Emotional Regulation / Emocinė savitvarda
   - Constructive Outlook / Konstruktyvus požiūris
   - Reflective Self-Awareness / Reflektyvi savimonė

Detailed construct rationale: `WORKSTYLE15_INDEPENDENT_MODEL_V01.md`.

This architecture uses broad personality/work-behaviour concepts as anchors but is **not yet psychometrically validated**.

### Current WorkStyle 15 pre-pilot package

Prepared:
- `WORKSTYLE15_INDEPENDENT_MODEL_V01.md` — construct model;
- `WORKSTYLE15_V01_LT_ITEMS.md` — 75 original LT items, 5 per dimension;
- `WORKSTYLE15_INTERPRETATIONS_LT.md` — 15 dimension interpretations + 5 domain summaries;
- `WORKSTYLE15_PAEI_DISC_CROSSWALK.md` — rebuilt optional correspondence lenses;
- `WORKSTYLE15_V01_FIXED_ORDER.md` — deterministic balanced 75-item pilot order;
- `WORKSTYLE15_DECISIONS.md` — active decision log.

Current scoring draft:
- Likert 1–5;
- one reverse-key item per dimension;
- 5 items per dimension, raw 5–25;
- `dimension_index = (sum - 5) / 20 * 100`;
- domain = average of its 3 dimension indexes;
- indexes are theoretical scale positions, **not population percentiles**.

### Result architecture

Primary result:
1. 15 measured WorkStyle dimensions;
2. 5 WorkStyle domains as summary layer;
3. optional outer `PAEI | DISC` lens.

Result language:
- describe tendencies, contributions and trade-offs;
- do not equate high with good or low with weak;
- do not assign a fixed personality type;
- PAEI/DISC remain correspondence lenses, not official hidden tests.

### Deferred lens

Jungian / Myers-Briggs-style preference mapping remains explicitly deferred after V1.

If ever revisited:
- optional lens only;
- no official MBTI claim or four-letter type derived from WorkStyle responses.

### Immediate gates before coding WorkStyle 15

1. Cognitive/language review of the 75 LT items with real users.
2. Final cross-loading review, especially:
   - Achievement Drive vs Goal Persistence;
   - Social Energy vs Persuasive Influence / Assertiveness;
   - Constructive Outlook vs Emotional Regulation.
3. Review reverse-item comprehension.
4. Freeze LT beta wording + version IDs.
5. Implement anonymous LT beta instrumentation.
6. Translate to EN only after LT wording is stable.
7. Collect enough pilot data before shortening toward 60 items or adding numeric PAEI/DISC lens scores.

## Future concept — Organization Development / PAEI

Separate product concept, not an extension of individual WorkStyle scores.

Core idea:
- multiple people rate observable organizational behaviour across functions/levels;
- measure organizational P/A/E/I-related capabilities;
- aggregate at team/unit/organization level;
- repeat periodically to show development direction;
- identify imbalances/tensions rather than one fixed label.

Important boundary:
**do not create an organization PAEI score by averaging employees' personal WorkStyle profiles.**
