# WorkStyle 8-axis — Cognitive Form Selection

Status: ITEM SELECTION / PRE-PROTOTYPE / NOT SCORED
Date: 2026-08-23

## Purpose

Reduce the oversized Stage 0 material into a practical first cognitive form.

Target:
- 5 stronger axes × 5 items = 25 items;
- 3 fragile axes × 3 challenge items = 9 items;
- total = **34 bipolar items**.

This is not a psychometric short form. It is a cognitive / construct-distinctness prototype.

No result profile should be shown yet.

---

# Selection principles

Retain items that:
- clearly belong to one axis;
- give both poles plausible value;
- use an observable work situation;
- avoid obvious competence/right-answer cues;
- avoid unnecessary role dependence;
- collectively cover more than one context within the axis.

Drop or park items that:
- are duplicates;
- cross-load onto another axis;
- make one answer objectively better;
- depend strongly on authority / role;
- measure pace, confidence, intelligence or communication skill instead of style.

# Axis 1 — Structured Preparation ↔ Emergent Organisation

## KEEP
- **A1-01** new, not very complex task: plan main steps ↔ start with first clear step and organise while working
- **A1-03** task done only a few times: create clearer work order ↔ let order emerge from first attempts
- **A1-04** details not all known: preliminary structure then refine ↔ leave more open and structure as details emerge
- **A1-05** outcome known / route free: prefer clearer sequence before start ↔ prefer sequence to emerge from work
- **A1-06** recurring but variable task: use base routine ↔ organise more around each situation

## PARK
- A1-02 — near-duplicate of A1-04/A1-05
- A1-07 — risks shifting into prioritisation rather than structure
- A1-08 — introduces start-speed / pace confound

---

# Axis 2 — Method Consistency ↔ Method Adaptation

## KEEP
- **A2-01** current method works but new suggestion appears
- **A2-02** conditions change slightly while old method still works
- **A2-04** shared method while circumstances gradually change
- **A2-06** functioning routine plus new tool/opportunity
- **A2-07** same work performed somewhat differently by several people

## PARK
- A2-03 — drifts toward Explore/Exploit / novelty preference
- A2-05 — one new signal risks overlap with Conclusion Revision
- A2-08 — near-duplicate novelty/exploration framing

Important cognitive check:
A2-04/A2-07 may be role-sensitive for people who do not work in teams. `Neteko tokios situacijos` is informative here.

---

# Axis 3 — Local / Concrete Focus ↔ System / Relational Focus

Fragile axis: retain only 3 challenge items.

## KEEP
- **B1-01** concrete location of a problem ↔ related parts of work
- **B1-03** limited time: narrow to key concrete area ↔ brief scan of several related areas
- **B1-06** small early change: observe concrete location ↔ check related locations

## PARK
- B1-02 — analytical-competence flavour
- B1-04 — too safety/role dependent
- B1-05 — causal reasoning ability contamination

Survival question:
Do respondents hear `zoom in ↔ zoom out`, or `simple thinker ↔ systems thinker`?

---

# Axis 4 — Conclusion Stability ↔ Evidence-led Revision

Highest-risk axis: retain only 3 challenge items.

## KEEP
- **B2-01** one credible new fact that does not fully fit an established conclusion
- **B2-03** repeated pattern with one non-fitting case
- **B2-05** new information partly contradicts prior conclusion

## PARK
- B2-02 — second pole may sound too explicitly scientific
- B2-04 — overlap with Analysis Level
- B2-06 — decisiveness / closure confound

Survival question:
Can respondents defend a higher threshold for revision as resistance to noise rather than stubbornness?

---

# Axis 5 — Opportunity Scanning ↔ Risk Scanning

Fragile axis: retain only 3 challenge items.

## KEEP
- **B3-01** new work idea with unclear benefit
- **B3-03** possible time saving with uncertainty
- **B3-04** planned change with little data

## PARK
- B3-02 — near-duplicate of B3-01/B3-04
- B3-05 — `pažeidžiamumas` wording too abstract
- B3-06 — colleague surface can introduce interpersonal trust

Survival question:
Do respondents answer about **first attention**, not willingness to take risk?

---

# Axis 6 — Autonomous Execution ↔ Collaborative Coordination

## KEEP
- **C1-01** task can be completed alone / help not necessary
- **C1-03** clear task and personal responsibility
- **C1-06** idea can first be checked independently
- **C1-07** work part not tightly coupled with others
- **C1-08** individual vs joint first pass at a problem

## PARK
- C1-02 — if colleagues are affected, coordination becomes normatively better
- C1-04 — information-seeking / efficiency confound
- C1-05 — handoff makes early coordination objectively useful

Important cognitive check:
Autonomy must read as efficient ownership, not lack of teamwork.

---

# Axis 7 — Direct Disagreement ↔ Alignment-seeking

## KEEP
- **C2-01** differing opinions, solution still needed
- **C2-02** proposal disliked but issue not critical
- **C2-04** partially disagree with another person's option
- **C2-07** disagreement over work method
- **C2-08** need to say another person's proposal does not fit

## PARK
- C2-03 — explicit tension may make alignment obviously desirable
- C2-05 — relationship damage framing biases toward softer response
- C2-06 — majority context introduces conformity / status effects

Important cognitive check:
The axis must be heard as **how disagreement enters the conversation**, not courage versus weakness.

---

# Axis 8 — Expressive Participation ↔ Reserved Participation

## KEEP
- **C3-01** ordinary group discussion with something to add
- **C3-02** informal work discussion
- **C3-06** thoughts available but not necessary to decision
- **C3-07** several people already speaking actively
- **C3-08** comfortable group context

## PARK
- C3-03 — starting discussion may confound leadership/initiative
- C3-04 — emotional expressiveness rather than participation
- C3-05 — unfamiliar group introduces social anxiety / shyness

Important cognitive check:
Reserved Participation must read as selective contribution, not disengagement.

# Prototype rules

The first 34-item prototype should:
- randomise/interleave axes rather than display eight labelled blocks;
- show no axis names during answering;
- show both anchors symmetrically;
- include `Priklauso nuo situacijos`;
- include `Neteko tokios situacijos / negaliu įvertinti`;
- capture item response and active response time;
- allow optional `Šitas klausimas neaiškus` flag;
- collect brief end feedback;
- show **no score/profile**.

Why no result yet:
Showing a profile would train the respondent to interpret constructs before we know that the constructs themselves survive cognitive testing.

---

# Primary cognitive outputs

Per item:
- scale position;
- context-dependent response rate;
- cannot-assess rate;
- unclear flag;
- response latency.

Per axis:
- whether items are paraphrased consistently as the intended axis;
- whether one pole is seen as clearly more desirable;
- whether neighbouring axes feel repetitive.

This form is ready to become a private / research prototype without claiming measurement validity.
