# WorkStyle construct audit 05 — Achievement Drive and Empathic Attention

Status: CONSTRUCT AUDIT / PRE-ITEM-WRITING
Date: 2026-08-23
Branch: hero-webgl

## Purpose

Test whether the two strongest remaining unipolar candidates deserve a place in the reduced WorkStyle architecture.

The question is not whether they are psychologically meaningful. The question is narrower:

> Do they provide distinctive, non-redundant WorkStyle information that justifies mixing unipolar scales with bipolar axes in the public core?

No new items are written here.

---

## 1. Achievement Drive / Pasiekimų siekis (`ACH`)

Current v0.6 items:
- `ACH-01` Dažnai renkuosi užduotis, kurios reikalauja iš manęs daugiau nei įprastai.
- `ACH-04` Kai rezultatas jau geras, dažnai ieškau, ką dar galima pagerinti.
- `ACH-05` Jei rezultatas pakankamai geras, dažniausiai ties tuo ir sustoju.

### Why it is not cleanly bipolar

`Achievement ↔ Sufficiency` is not a defensible single continuum.

A person may:
- seek ambitious goals on high-value work;
- stop improving low-value work once it is sufficient;
- be highly driven but highly selective about where effort is spent.

Therefore `sufficiency` can reflect prioritisation or efficiency rather than low achievement orientation.

### What the current items mix

The current trio combines at least two tendencies:
1. challenge seeking / aspiration;
2. improvement threshold / willingness to continue optimising.

These may correlate but are not identical.

### WorkStyle value

Achievement orientation is relevant to workplace behaviour and is not a pure competence. However it has two disadvantages for this product philosophy:
- strong social-desirability gradient (`more achievement` reads as better);
- overlap with Goal Persistence / commitment and potentially with pace/effort.

### Decision

**DO NOT force ACH into a bipolar axis.**

**Retain only as a provisional unipolar candidate, not guaranteed core.**

Working label:

`Challenge / improvement drive`

Status: PROVISIONAL UNIPOLAR.

Gate for survival:
- must show discriminant value from Goal Persistence;
- must not collapse into simple socially desirable endorsement;
- public interpretation must avoid `higher = better` language.

If architecture pressure requires reduction, ACH is a plausible drop candidate before the strong bipolar core axes.

## 2. Empathic Attention / Empatinis dėmesys (`EMP`)

Current v0.6 items:
- `EMP-01` Jei kolega sureaguoja kitaip, nei tikėjausi, paklausiu, kaip jis tai suprato.
- `EMP-02` Prieš sprendimą, kuris palies kitus, pagalvoju, kaip jie į jį gali reaguoti.
- `EMP-05` Darbo sprendimuose dažniausiai remiuosi tuo, ką žmonės pasako tiesiai, o ne jų emocinėmis reakcijomis.

### Bipolarity problem

`Empathy ↔ factual / explicit cues` is not a true opposite.

A person can be highly attentive to:
- emotional/interpersonal signals;
- explicit facts and statements

at the same time.

The current reverse item therefore creates an artificial trade-off and was already identified as construct-distorting in the cognitive pilot.

### What the current items actually ask

The direct items mix:
- perspective checking;
- anticipating interpersonal impact.

Those behaviours may reflect empathy, social awareness, communication skill, or learned managerial practice.

### WorkStyle vs competence problem

Unlike `Cooperation ↔ Autonomy` or `Assertive ↔ Accommodating`, EMP has no clearly legitimate opposite pole.

Low EMP is also difficult to describe non-evaluatively. In a workplace instrument it easily reads as:
- insensitive;
- inattentive to people;
- socially less competent.

That makes the construct especially vulnerable to desirability and impression management.

### Relationship to the future DISC/IPC lens

The interpersonal core already contains stronger stylistic information:
- Cooperation ↔ Autonomy;
- Assertive ↔ Accommodating;
- Social Energy ↔ Reserved Participation.

Those axes describe interpersonal **style** more directly. EMP adds a more evaluative social-perception construct and may not provide enough incremental value.

### Decision

**DROP Empathic Attention from the reduced WorkStyle core.**

Do not replace it with `facts ↔ feelings` or similar false bipolarity.

Possible future locations:
- leadership competence / SJT;
- structured interview;
- development-only reflection prompts.

## 3. Hybrid-model implication

After this audit:

- ACH remains the only strong candidate for a genuinely unipolar WorkStyle construct from this pair;
- EMP is removed from the core.

Therefore a hybrid model is still possible, but the burden of proof shifts:

> The architecture should not retain unipolar scales merely because the original 15 contained them.

If the final core contains only one or two unipolar constructs, product simplicity may later favour either:
- keeping them as explicitly different measurement families; or
- moving them into an optional secondary layer.

That is a later architecture decision, not an item-writing decision.
