# WorkStyle 15 — two-review consensus on bipolar methodology

Status: METHODOLOGY CONSENSUS / PRE-REDESIGN
Date: 2026-08-23

Reviewer identities are intentionally left unattributed until confirmed by the user. The two reviews are labelled A and B.

## Executive consensus

Both independent reviewers reached the same top-level verdict:

**PROCEED WITH HYBRID MODEL**

- Reviewer A confidence: 72%
- Reviewer B confidence: 68%

Neither reviewer supports converting all 15 inherited WorkStyle dimensions into bipolar axes. Neither supports simply returning to a pure unipolar design. Both require empirical validation of bipolarity per construct.

The consensus is therefore stronger than either review alone: **bipolarity is a per-axis hypothesis, not an architecture-wide assumption.**

## Strongest areas of agreement

### Strong bipolar consensus
Both reviewers independently classify these as strong bipolar candidates:
- Work Structure: Structure ↔ Improvisation
- Adaptability: Adaptability ↔ Consistency/Stability
- Cooperation: Cooperation ↔ Autonomy
- Assertive Communication: Assertive ↔ Accommodating/Harmony
- Social Energy: Social/active participation ↔ Reserved participation

These five form the most defensible initial bipolar core for Stage 0 review.

### Strong unipolar / split consensus
Both reviewers agree these should not be forced into simple bipolar axes:
- Achievement Drive
- Empathic Attention
- Emotional Regulation
- Reflective Self-Awareness

Shared reasons:
- the proposed opposite may be a separate legitimate construct;
- both tendencies can coexist at high levels;
- midpoint becomes uninterpretable if high-high and low-low collapse together.

### Conditional / disputed constructs
Both see bipolar potential but require redesign or evidence for:
- Goal Persistence
- Learning Orientation
- Intellectual Humility
- Constructive Outlook

Reviewer B is more conservative on Systems Thinking and Persuasive Influence:
- Systems Thinking: A = strong bipolar; B = possible bipolar only as attentional/default level-of-analysis preference, not ability.
- Persuasive Influence: A = possible bipolar; B = unipolar / likely redundant with Assertive Communication.

These disagreements should become explicit Stage 0 test questions.

## Consensus methodological principles

1. **Do not preserve 15 because of history.** The construct count is provisional.
2. **Do not write v0.7 yet.** Resolve construct/pole definitions and redundancy first.
3. **Bipolarity must be demonstrated empirically.** Semantic opposites are insufficient.
4. **Use a hybrid response architecture if constructs survive review:** semantic differential only for genuinely bipolar axes; typicality scale for unipolar constructs.
5. **Midpoint must not carry context dependence.** Both reviewers recommend a separate `Depends on the situation` response.
6. **Cannot assess / no experience remains separate missing data.** Never convert to midpoint.
7. **No respondent-facing numerical scores.** Directional language / neutral zones only.
8. **No numerical domain averages** until higher-order structure is empirically demonstrated.
9. **No filled radar polygon.** It implies magnitude / outward-is-better. Prefer bipolar bars or neutral-ring markers.
10. **DISC and PAEI remain correspondence lenses, not WorkStyle architecture.** No hand mapping and no claims of equivalence.

## Architectural implication

The current 5 × 3 = 15 structure should now be treated as an inherited candidate taxonomy, not a frozen architecture.

Reviewer A leaves open roughly 15 constructs under a hybrid treatment.
Reviewer B explicitly recommends a reduced target of approximately 9–11 constructs and suggests:
- merging or testing Assertive Communication vs Persuasive Influence redundancy;
- dropping Reflective Self-Awareness as a scored style construct;
- splitting Emotional Regulation into reactivity and expressiveness candidates;
- considering missing work-style constructs such as pace/tempo, conflict engagement, and detail-vs-gist processing.

Consensus action: **perform a construct-architecture review before item development.**

## Response-format consensus

For bipolar axes:
- initially test 5 semantic-differential positions;
- both pole labels always visible;
- midpoint = both sides equally descriptive / balanced;
- separate `Depends on the situation`;
- separate `Cannot assess / no relevant experience`.

For unipolar constructs:
- workplace typicality response format;
- reverse items only where they represent the same construct rather than another legitimate preference.

Internal signed coding can be used for bipolar research data, but should be treated as ordinal and should not appear to respondents.

## Item-development consensus

Do not reduce immediately to three final items per construct.

Use a larger candidate bank first (roughly 8–12 candidates per construct/axis) across different concrete work situations, then reduce after:
- cognitive interviews;
- desirability review;
- response distributions;
- structural evidence;
- redundancy/local-dependence analysis.

No respondent should need to answer the entire candidate bank; use rotated forms if necessary.

## Validation gate

Reviewers differ on exact sample sizes, but agree on sequence:
1. expert construct/pole review;
2. cognitive interviews;
3. usability/item-distribution pilot;
4. structural validation using ordinal/polychoric methods;
5. test–retest and convergent/discriminant evidence;
6. only then external DISC/PAEI correspondence exploration and hold-out validation.

Reviewer B places the structural go/no-go gate around N=300–500; Reviewer A gave an initial structural range around 150–250 followed by 400–600+ larger validation. For planning purposes, treat **N≈300+ as the safer structural target**, not a current prerequisite for Stage 0–2.

## DISC consensus

- Do not manually assign WorkStyle constructs to D/i/S/C.
- DISC-type correspondence is feasible only as an empirical / qualitative lens.
- Any quantitative projection requires external benchmark data and out-of-sample stability.
- Never present WorkStyle as official DiSC or equivalent.

## PAEI consensus

- Do not make P+A+E+I sum to 100%.
- Treat PAEI as overlapping qualitative projections.
- Reviewer B is more restrictive about future numeric PAEI mapping because of the target framework's own psychometric limitations.
- Current safe decision: qualitative lens only.
