# WorkStyle construct audit 03 — Systems Thinking vs Learning Orientation

Status: STAGE 0 / CONSTRUCT AUDIT / PRE-ITEM-WRITING
Date: 2026-08-23

## Purpose

Examine whether the current WorkStyle constructs:
- `Systems Thinking / Sisteminis mąstymas` (SYS), and
- `Learning Orientation / Mokymosi orientacija` (LRN)

represent separable work-style constructs, overlap substantially, or should be redefined / reduced.

No new item bank is authorised by this audit.

---

## 1. What the current v0.6 items actually ask

### SYS — Systems Thinking

Current items:
- SYS-01: when a problem repeats, look for what causes it;
- SYS-03: when changing one part of work, consider what it changes elsewhere;
- SYS-05R: when a problem appears one-off, solve it locally without looking for broader causes.

The item family mixes:
- causal analysis;
- downstream-effects awareness;
- breadth / level of analysis.

The strongest style-like core is not `ability to think systemically`, but:

> **the default level / breadth at which a person frames a work problem.**

Possible style space:

`local / concrete focus  ↔  broader relational / system focus`

Both can be legitimate depending on time, risk and problem type.

Main risk:
If items say that a broader analysis is objectively required, the construct becomes competence rather than preference.

---

### LRN — Learning Orientation

Current items:
- LRN-02: ask people who see the decision differently;
- LRN-04: after an error, change something concrete so it does not repeat;
- LRN-05R: if a work method produces good results, usually do not change it.

These three items do **not** share one clean behavioural referent.

They respectively tap:
- seeking disconfirming / external input;
- corrective learning after error;
- preference for proven methods versus changing them.

They therefore cross-load conceptually onto several other candidate constructs:
- LRN-02 → Intellectual Humility / Position Revision + Cooperation;
- LRN-04 → Reflection / feedback use + problem solving;
- LRN-05 → Method Consistency / Adaptation and Explore–Exploit.

Conclusion:
**current LRN is construct-heterogeneous.**

---

## 2. Are SYS and LRN one construct?

### Decision: NO — do not merge SYS and LRN by default

Conceptually they concern different questions.

SYS asks:

> **At what level do I tend to represent / inspect the problem?**

LRN, if redesigned as Explore–Exploit, would ask:

> **How readily do I prefer new / untested approaches versus established / proven approaches?**

A person can coherently be:
- strongly system-focused but strongly exploitative / method-conservative;
- locally focused but highly exploratory;
- high or low on both tendencies depending on context.

Therefore the constructs are not logical opposites and should not be forced into one axis.

---

## 3. SYS as a WorkStyle candidate

### Working redesign

Possible conceptual label:

**Level of Analysis**

Candidate poles:

`Local / concrete focus  ↔  System / relational focus`

### Working definition

> The person's usual tendency to initially frame work issues at the immediate/local level versus in terms of dependencies, recurring patterns and effects across a wider system.

### Bipolarity status

**POSSIBLE / STRONG-ISH BIPOLAR CANDIDATE, but not yet accepted.**

Why it is promising:
- the two poles occupy the same attentional / framing space;
- both can be functional;
- a local focus can support speed, specificity and containment;
- a broader systems focus can support pattern recognition and downstream-effect awareness.

Why it is not automatically strong:
- `systems thinking` is often treated as an ability / competence;
- global–local processing research suggests that apparent processing styles can be task-dependent and not always stable across measurement paradigms;
- therefore the WorkStyle version must explicitly measure **default attentional framing**, not capability.

### Design constraint

Do not ask:
`Do I notice the wider consequences?`

That invites a competence / socially desirable answer.

Instead future items must create situations in which both a narrow immediate focus and a broader relational scan are credible first moves.

---

## 4. LRN should not survive in its current form

### Current decision

**DROP `Learning Orientation` as currently defined.**

Reason:
The label suggests one construct, but the item bank currently measures three different processes.

This does not mean learning-related style is irrelevant.

A cleaner replacement hypothesis may be:

### Explore ↔ Exploit

Candidate poles:

`Proven-method reliance  ↔  Novel-method exploration`

Working definition:

> The person's usual preference for drawing primarily on established, known approaches versus deliberately trying alternatives with less certain outcomes when more than one method is viable.

This is a recognised decision tension in organisational and behavioural research, but it should not be imported uncritically as a stable trait.

Status:
**CONDITIONAL BIPOLAR CANDIDATE / NOT YET ACCEPTED.**

---

## 5. Critical overlap: Explore–Exploit vs Method Adaptation

This is now the more important redundancy question than SYS vs LRN.

From Audit 01:

`Method Consistency ↔ Method Adaptation`

Possible distinction:

### Explore–Exploit
When choosing among viable approaches:
- rely on an established method;
- explore a less-proven alternative.

### Method Consistency–Adaptation
Once an approach is already in use:
- preserve it despite changing conditions / feedback;
- modify it as conditions / feedback evolve.

This distinction is conceptually possible:
- **selection of method** versus
- **updating of method**.

But it may be too subtle for a short self-report WorkStyle instrument.

Therefore Explore–Exploit must not be admitted as a final axis until cognitive testing shows respondents distinguish it from Method Adaptation.

Potential outcomes:
1. both survive as separate axes;
2. they merge into one broader `Method stability ↔ exploration/adaptation` axis;
3. Explore–Exploit is dropped because Method Adaptation captures the useful variance more cleanly.

---

## 6. Relationship to Intellectual Humility / Position Revision

LRN-02 currently asks whether the respondent seeks people who see a decision differently.

That is better interpreted as evidence around:
- willingness to expose a position to counter-evidence;
- revision threshold;
- external input seeking.

It should not be used to define a general Learning Orientation scale.

The future Intellectual Humility audit should decide whether `Position Revision Threshold` survives as a style axis.

---

## 7. Relationship to Reflective Self-Awareness

LRN-04 currently asks for a behavioural change after an error.

This can reflect:
- learning;
- accountability;
- problem-solving skill;
- reflection;
- process discipline.

It is too broad to anchor a WorkStyle learning dimension.

If Reflective Self-Awareness is later dropped as a scored construct, post-event learning behaviours may still appear in the product as interpretation content without needing a separate dimension.

---

## 8. External theoretical check

### Global / local framing

Research distinguishes global versus local processing and shows links between processing level and dynamic-system understanding. However, newer work also warns that different global/local tasks do not necessarily measure one stable unitary trait.

Implication:
`Local ↔ System focus` is a plausible WorkStyle hypothesis, not an established stable trait. It needs context-controlled cognitive and structural testing.

### Explore / exploit

Organisational and decision research treats exploration versus exploitation as a genuine trade-off between trying uncertain alternatives and using known, predictable options.

However, exploration/exploitation behaviour is affected by both individual differences and situational context.

Implication:
The axis is conceptually plausible, but WorkStyle must test whether it behaves as a stable enough preference to justify individual reflection feedback.

---

## 9. Architecture impact

After three construct audits:

### Strong / promising bipolar candidates retained
- Method Consistency ↔ Method Adaptation (from ADP)
- Position Assertion ↔ Accommodation (from AST)
- Level of Analysis: Local ↔ System focus (SYS, provisional)

### Conditional bipolar candidates
- Commitment Stability ↔ Goal Reallocation (PER redesign)
- Explore ↔ Exploit (possible LRN replacement)

### Dropped as inherited scored constructs
- Persuasive Influence (INF)
- Learning Orientation as currently defined (LRN)

Important:
Dropping LRN does not necessarily reduce final count by one because Explore–Exploit may replace it. The final construct count remains evidence-driven.

---

## 10. Current decision

**SYS:** RETAIN / REDEFINE as `Local ↔ System level of analysis`, provisional bipolar WorkStyle candidate.

**LRN:** DROP in current form.

**Explore ↔ Exploit:** retain only as a replacement hypothesis, pending direct comparison with `Method Consistency ↔ Method Adaptation`.

**SYS + LRN merge:** REJECTED conceptually.

Do not write final items yet.

Recommended next construct audit:

`Emotional Regulation / Constructive Outlook / Reflective Self-Awareness`

Reason:
This is the most methodologically unstable remaining cluster: the current three constructs mix emotional recovery, optimism/risk scanning, post-event reflection and self-awareness, and both independent reviews questioned whether several of these are WorkStyle dimensions at all.
