What it explores

Mirror is designed for people who influence others and want to ask a simple question: could some of my own behaviour habits occasionally slow down my growth?

It does not try to define your personality. It reflects patterns across six behaviour areas and gives you open questions to sit with.

How the reflection works

Status & need for recognitionHow strongly being seen, acknowledged or valued can shape your reactions.
Being right, control & accountabilityHow you respond when decisions, responsibility or control are contested.
Listening & making spaceHow much room you leave for other voices, especially when you already have a view.
Recognizing & thanking othersHow consistently appreciation becomes visible in your behaviour.
Emotional reactionsHow tension, frustration or pressure may change the way you respond.
Flexibility & sharingHow easily you adjust, share ownership and let other approaches coexist.

Read the result as a mirror

The six scores are not verdicts. The tool highlights 2–3 areas that may deserve more awareness and pairs each with a reflection question.

This is a self-reflection tool, not a psychological test, diagnosis, 360° assessment or competency evaluation.

Privacy

No registration. No server-side storage. Answers stay in your browser, and PDF export uses the browser print dialog.

Ready to look again?
