Leadership 360°

What it is

Leadership 360° is a multi-rater leadership development prototype. A leader compares self-perception with feedback from people around them: manager, peers, direct reports and other relevant colleagues.

The current build supports Lithuanian and English, pseudonymous invitations, a separated response collector and a dedicated Guardian administration space. The Guardian manages invitations, completion status and cycles, but does not see individual answers, comments, the leader report or the 90-day plan.

The development cycle

1 · Set up C1The assessed leader defines the Guardian and evaluator groups for the first cycle.
2 · Collect perspectivesSelf, Manager, Peer, Direct Report and Other groups answer 75 behaviour-frequency statements across 15 competencies.
3 · Compare the viewThe leader report shows competency and cluster results, strengths and self-versus-others gaps while anonymity thresholds protect small groups.
4 · Build a 90-day planThe result can be translated into a structured development plan with a small number of selected actions.
5 · Run C2 and look againA repeat cycle measures what changed and can generate an adapted next plan.

Privacy by role

Identity and response data are kept separate. Evaluators receive their own invitation context, while the Guardian sees only operational information such as who was invited, whether a response is complete and which cycle is active.

Reports, individual answers, open-text comments, 90-day plans and C1–C2 comparisons belong outside the Guardian administration role. Small feedback groups are suppressed or merged when anonymity would be too weak.

Between C1 and C2

The 90-day plan is not intended to disappear after the report is closed. Selected actions can be exported to a calendar and passed to the Telegram companion so that the development work remains visible between measurements.

Only selected development actions are passed to the companion. Raw 360° answers, evaluator identities and open comments are not.

Organisation layer

The prototype also explores an organisation-level analytics layer: programme participation, completion, recurring competency patterns, self-versus-others gaps and C1→C2 movement can be analysed only from aggregated data.

The purpose is not to diagnose individuals, but to help identify where an organisation may need development, cultural attention or a closer look at systems and practices.

Positioning

This is a larger professional-development prototype within the 2rasi ecosystem rather than a short self-reflection experiment. The public prototype is used to validate the full cycle before a future organisation-ready product layer.

Leadership 360° is intended for development and reflection, not diagnosis or automated decisions about a person.
