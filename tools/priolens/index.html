Architecture in development

PrioLens

Two valid directions compete. Repeated choices can make the pattern of priority easier to see.

New 2rasi product 8 working directions 28 pairwise comparisons Not yet open for participation

What is being built

PrioLens is being designed to help a person see relative priorities more clearly when two meaningful directions cannot both win at the same moment.

The first protocol will compare every one of eight working directions with every other direction once, using more than one visual exemplar per direction.

What it will not claim

The current design does not claim to diagnose personality, reveal the subconscious or turn response speed into a psychological score. The first result is intended to be a transparent trace of relative choices and a prompt for reflection.

Not open yet

The route and protocol architecture are being prepared first. The pilot will open only after the stimulus set, session planner and measured rendering rules are frozen.
