Formative research prototype

PrioLens

Three images at a time. Choose what pulls you first. Then compare that pattern with how sufficient several areas of life feel right now.

14 visual choices 12 current-sufficiency ratings ~3 minutes Reflection, not diagnosis
Start formative pilot →

What happens

First you see 14 sets of three images without need labels or explanations. In each set, choose the image that pulls you first. If none does, say so.

Only after the visual part, you rate how sufficient several broad areas feel in your life right now. These are two separate perspectives, not one hidden score.

What the result shows

The result highlights themes that repeated across different images, shows your current sufficiency landscape, and points out places where the two perspectives are worth looking at together.

A repeated visual choice does not mean that something is missing. A high sufficiency rating does not mean a theme should stop attracting you. The interesting part is the relationship between the two.

What it does not claim

PrioLens does not diagnose personality, reveal a subconscious or 'true self', rank your needs, or treat a faster response as more truthful. This version is a formative research prototype for reflection and stimulus research.

Look once. Then look again.
