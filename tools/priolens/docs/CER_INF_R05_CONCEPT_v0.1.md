# CER ↔ INF R05 concept v0.1

Status: TEXT-ONLY CANDIDATE / BLIND REVIEW REQUIRED
Date: 2026-08-31

## Purpose

Replace failed MAS↔INF and PRO↔INF third-opponent attempts in INF Gate D1 with a lower-valence CER↔INF contrast.

Working target definitions (hidden from Stage 1 reviewer):
- CER · Certainty / Aiškumas: clarity, predictability or knowing what applies, what is present, or what is likely to happen.
- INF · Influence / Poveikis: meaningful impact on the direction, decision or outcome of an external/shared situation.

## Shared context

A neutral tabletop channel/flow board in a plain workshop-like setting. One hand interacts with the same central area in both variants. Two equal downstream routes and the same small neutral moving pieces/tokens are present. Same camera, lighting, materials, route count, object count and overall visual salience. No text, labels, color coding, reward, hazard, status cues or attractive destination difference.

## Variant A · hidden CER

The hand slides aside one small frosted inspection cover over the central junction. Under it, the existing internal connection/path is directly visible through a clear window. The currently active route can be seen unambiguously from the physical geometry itself. Nothing in the flow is redirected and no destination changes.

The reveal is modest and complete enough to show the current state, not staged as mystery, surprise or discovery of unfamiliar contents.

## Variant B · hidden INF

The same hand moves one ordinary central junction piece in the same board. The visible geometry of the junction changes so that the same existing flow now follows the other equal downstream route. Total amount, speed, route count and destination attractiveness remain unchanged.

The internal state remains equally legible; the distinctive consequence is that the course of the external flow changes.

## Known risks

- A may still read as reveal / inspection / EXP rather than CER.
- B may still read as generic routing/causality rather than INF.
- B may appear more consequential/active than A.
- If Stage 1 reads the pair mainly as `inspection vs operation` rather than `state legibility vs directional intervention`, this edge should not advance.

## Gate rule

One blind Stage 1 + one reveal Stage 2 only. Do not iterate indefinitely.
