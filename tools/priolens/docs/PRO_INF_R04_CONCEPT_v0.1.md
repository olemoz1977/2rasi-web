# PrioLens Gate D1 · R04 PRO↔INF concept v0.1

Status: TEXT-ONLY CANDIDATE / REPLACES MAS↔INF AS THIRD INF OPPONENT
Date: 2026-08-30

## Purpose

Test INF cross-edge identity against a third opponent after MAS↔INF was stopped following repeated construct/confound tradeoffs.

Working hidden edge:
- PRO · Protection: preventing harm/loss, reducing exposure, shielding or preserving something that matters.
- INF · Influence: meaningful impact on the direction, decision or outcome of an external/shared situation.

## Shared context

An ordinary outdoor garden/work area with two equal neutral planting beds and one simple central irrigation manifold between them. One worker is shown only from shoulders/hands downward at the manifold. Same camera, lighting, clothing, plant density, water amount, object count and overall visual attractiveness in both variants. No lush harvest, flowers, food, danger, logos or text.

Both planting beds are equally healthy and equally ordinary. Neither is framed as more valuable, needy or rewarding.

## Variant A · hidden PRO

The worker uses both hands to close and secure a simple fitted protective cover around the central irrigation manifold. A mild, low-arousal environmental exposure is visible: small wind-blown dry leaves / light dust are present around the area, and the cover physically prevents them from entering the exposed moving junctions. The water route and distribution remain unchanged.

Constraints:
- no storm, darkness, damage, emergency or heroic pose;
- no expensive or high-tech enclosure;
- no lock/security symbolism;
- protection is visible through barrier/interception, not inferred from story.

## Variant B · hidden INF

The same worker uses both hands on one simple central routing gate in the same manifold. The same modest water flow is visibly redirected from one equal planting bed to the other. During the visible moment, one bed receives the shared flow and the other does not; total water amount remains fixed. Neither bed becomes greener, fuller or otherwise rewarded.

Constraints:
- no larger effect, more beds or more water;
- no authority/status cue;
- no passive followers;
- consequence is the changed external allocation of a fixed shared resource, not generic mechanism alone.

## Why this edge

This pair deliberately tests INF in a non-social external system after R01/R03 supported INF in social/shared contexts. If the same broader idea survives here, cross-edge identity evidence becomes more diverse.

## Main risks

- PRO may still attract preference through safety/care even without threat drama.
- INF may still collapse to generic irrigation control/routing.
- dry leaves/dust may add nuisance salience if over-rendered.
- mutually exclusive water allocation must be visible enough to feel consequential without implying that one destination is better.

## Decision rule

Run blind Stage 1 first. If the reviewer sees only `cover vs valve` or `maintenance vs routing` and does not identify a meaningful external allocation consequence, do not force the reveal interpretation. If Stage 2 still classifies INF as generic control/causality rather than meaningful external consequence, stop this concept.
