## Working CER impulse

The current intended CER impulse is:

> **Trauka į būseną, kurioje aišku, kas vyksta ar ko tikėtis.**

This is deliberately narrower than precision, completion, tidiness, safety, permission or technical correctness.

## Result

CER currently has **no strong standalone image** whose first impulse is clearly the intended CER impulse.

The green traffic light is at best a weak HOLD because it expresses a known permission state through a culturally learned symbol.

## Why this may be structural

`Certainty / predictability` is partly temporal and inferential: a viewer needs to know what will happen next, what a signal means, or that a state is stable.

A single static photograph often expresses only a proxy:
- order;
- completion;
- precision;
- safety/security;
- permission;
- visible contents;
- a clearly marked route.

Those proxies can be useful in their own right, but they should not be silently called CER if the first impulse is something else.

## Decision rule before further generation

Do not generate more CER images merely to fill the eight-direction bank.

Before any new CER generation, decide whether the intended construct should remain:

> certainty / clarity / predictability

or whether a more visually direct motivational hypothesis such as **stability / knownness / clear permission / structure** should replace or narrow it.

Until that decision is made, CER is **CONSTRUCT HOLD**, not an image-production gap.

This does not remove CER permanently. It prevents semantic rescue and quota-filling.
