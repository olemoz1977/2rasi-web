# F8 Local configuration/control stimulus 01 v0.1

Status: CONCEPT / ONE-STIMULUS EXPERIMENT / NOT BROAD-DIRECTION EVIDENCE
Date: 2026-08-31

## Family under test

**F8 — Local configuration/control**

Working visible definition:
> An actor directly changes the configuration of an immediately used object or local setup for their own use, with no visible downstream effect on a wider external system.

This is a lower-level semantic family. It is **not** evidence for AUT / Autonomy.

## Why this concept

Existing F8-adjacent Gallery material is concentrated in workstation/control-panel contexts. A new exemplar should change domain while preserving the local-configuration mechanism.

Chosen scene:
> A person adjusts the length of one shoulder strap on a plain backpack using the strap slider/buckle.

Why it is useful:
- local effect only;
- direct configuration action is visible;
- no wider system consequence;
- no route/path/door trope;
- no explicit choice between options;
- low technical expertise requirement;
- materially different from workstation and control-panel scenes.

## Main confounds to watch

- travel/preparation interpretation;
- comfort/fit rather than generic local configuration;
- consumer-product attractiveness;
- hands/action salience.

Reject the concept if the first reading is mainly "packing for travel", "getting ready", "comfort", or "choosing a backpack" rather than adjusting/configuring the object.

## Image constraints

- one photorealistic square 1:1 scene;
- close enough that the strap slider action is instantly readable on a small phone screen;
- plain neutral backpack, no brand/logo;
- only one adjustment action;
- neutral indoor background;
- no travel scenery, map, suitcase, door, path or vehicle;
- no text, labels, symbols, UI, arrows or infographic cues;
- no before/after, split screen or paired comparison;
- neutral lighting and moderate contrast;
- no dramatic aesthetics or success/reward staging.

## Generation prompt

Create one photorealistic square 1:1 image. Show a plain neutral backpack in close view, with one person's hands clearly adjusting the length of a single shoulder strap through a simple sliding buckle. The visible action must be direct local configuration of the backpack for use: one hand steadies the strap while the other pulls the loose webbing through the buckle. Keep the backpack generic, functional and unbranded. Use a neutral indoor setting with simple natural lighting and no travel context. Do not show a suitcase, map, road, doorway, vehicle, hiking scenery or other journey cues. Do not show multiple options or a choice between objects. No text, labels, logos, symbols, arrows, diagrams, collage, split screen, before-and-after layout or infographic elements. The composition must have one clear readable action and work at small mobile-screen size.

## Evaluation question after render

Before assigning any legacy label, ask only:
> What is visibly happening here in one short sentence?

Desired spontaneous description should stay close to:
- "adjusting the strap";
- "changing the fit/setting of the backpack";
- "configuring it for use".

Do not count acceptance of AUT/Autonomy as success criterion.
