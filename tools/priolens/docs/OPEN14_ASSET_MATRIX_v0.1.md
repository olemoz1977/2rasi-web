## Family-specific notes

### REST

The two exemplars represent different restoration mechanisms:
- stopping/resting;
- replenishing/refreshing.

This is useful diversity, but drink imagery has basic reward salience.

### RESOURCE

Harvest and gift are intentionally vivid. Their salience is not automatically a reason to reject them. The later question is whether RESOURCE is systematically more visually rewarding than other families.

### SAFETY

Personal shielding vs object safeguarding gives useful mechanism diversity.

### ORDER

Both assets involve arrangement/visibility. A future third mechanism could use sequencing or alignment, but there is no need to generate it now.

### CONNECTION

Dyadic social contact vs reciprocal action. Do not interpret either as CARE.

### CONTROL

Flow routing vs personal/physical setting control. This is one of the clearest newly recovered families that was previously spread across INF/PRO/CER.

### MASTERY

Still craft/precision heavy, but the semantic first read is strong enough for the first broad scan.

### KNOWLEDGE

Classroom and information-access scenes are materially different, but both may carry education/reading familiarity. Keep as research candidates.

### OPPORTUNITY

Prepared materials vs blank canvas separate `ready resource` from `open possibility`, but RESOURCE remains a close neighbor. Their later separation is empirical, not assumed.

## Six open prototype mechanisms

These are text-only mechanism placeholders from the viability probe. They are **not image-generation prompts yet**.

### CARE-A
One person gives physical cover/protection to another while accepting a small visible cost themselves.

### CARE-B
One-way tending/support of another person's immediate need.

### RECOGNITION-A
One person's contribution is visibly acknowledged by a small peer group; social acknowledgement is the event, not a trophy.

### RECOGNITION-B
One peer is clearly singled out for appreciation/congratulation within an otherwise equal group.

### SUCCESS-A
Clear goal endpoint reached, e.g. crossing a finish, without applause/trophy dominating.

### SUCCESS-B
Objective task/result visibly reaches completion/target, with the actor present but technical skill not dominating.
