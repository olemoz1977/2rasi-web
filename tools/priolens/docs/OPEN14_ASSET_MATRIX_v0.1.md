## Family-specific notes

### REST

The two exemplars represent different restoration mechanisms:
- stopping/resting;
- replenishing/refreshing.

Drink imagery has basic reward salience; track rather than sterilize.

### RESOURCE

Harvest and gift are intentionally vivid. Harvest is a saturation / edge-density outlier, but the first read is strong. Existing prepared-food alternatives replace one nuisance cue with another appetitive cue, so no automatic swap is justified.

### SAFETY

Personal shielding vs object safeguarding gives useful mechanism diversity.

### ORDER

Both assets involve arrangement/visibility. A future third mechanism could use sequencing or alignment, but there is no need to generate it now.

### CONNECTION

Dyadic social contact vs reciprocal action. Do not interpret either as CARE.

### AUTONOMY

Tool choice vs personal fit/configuration gives materially different mechanisms. The recovered audio control remains useful but cross-loads naturally with CONTROL.

### CONTROL

Flow routing vs changing a broader environment state is now both semantically diverse and visually less imbalanced than the previous pair.

### MASTERY

Still craft/precision heavy, but the semantic first read is strong enough for the first broad scan.

### KNOWLEDGE / LEARNING / UNDERSTANDING

A is explicit learning. B is deliberately a broader understanding/seeing-more boundary test and fixes the previous family-level portrait-only presentation issue. Preserve exemplar identity and do not merge their meanings post hoc if participant behavior diverges.

### OPPORTUNITY

Prepared materials vs blank canvas separate `ready resource` from `open possibility`. B is bright but not enough to justify a semantic downgrade to a darker greenhouse scene merely for balance.

## Six open prototype mechanisms

These are text-only mechanism placeholders from the viability probe. They are **not image-generation prompts yet**.

### CARE-A
One person gives physical cover/protection to another while accepting a small visible cost themselves.

### CARE-B
One-way tending/support of another person's immediate need.

### RECOGNITION-A
One person's contribution is visibly acknowledged by a small peer group; social acknowledgement is the event, not a trophy.

### RECOGNITION-B
One peer is clearly singled out for appreciation/congratulation within an otherwise equal group.

### SUCCESS-A
Clear goal endpoint reached, e.g. crossing a finish, without applause/trophy dominating.

### SUCCESS-B
Objective task/result visibly reaches completion/target, with the actor present but technical skill not dominating.
