## Decision

The 14-image historical P3 bank is no longer the active research bank.

It remains useful only as a completed technical/diagnostic checkpoint.

Next bank target:
- 7 active non-CER directions;
- 4 standalone exemplars per direction;
- 28 unique stimuli total;
- 28 three-image trials per owner research session;
- every stimulus shown exactly 3 times;
- every stimulus shown exactly once in each of the three screen slots;
- every direction shown 12 times;
- every one of the 21 direction pairs co-occurs exactly 4 times;
- `no_clear_choice` retained;
- latency retained as UX/process telemetry only.

This is a bank-expansion experiment, not a validation claim.

## Why 28 trials

With 7 directions, a seven-triad Fano round exposes every direction pair exactly once and every direction exactly three times.

Four independent balanced rounds therefore give:
- 28 triads;
- 84 total image exposures;
- 12 exposures per direction;
- 4 encounters per direction pair.

With four exemplars per direction, each exemplar can be shown exactly three times and exactly once per slot.

This gives a clean stimulus-level diagnostic while keeping the session compatible with the current ~2–4 minute product target, subject to owner timing checks.

## Result diagnostics

Direction-level summary remains secondary.

The primary research output must show, for every stimulus:
- shown count;
- chosen count;
- selection rate;
- slot exposure audit;
- competitors faced;
- wins by competitor context;
- round distribution;
- latency distribution when chosen and when not chosen.

Direction aggregation must preserve exemplar identity. A direction should be treated as suspect when one exemplar dominates while its siblings repeatedly fail.
