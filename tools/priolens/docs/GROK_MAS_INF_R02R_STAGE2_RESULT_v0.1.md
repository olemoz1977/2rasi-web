## Methodological interpretation

R02R solved the **confound problem** but created a **construct dilution problem**.

This is evidence that exact-mechanism matching can be too aggressive for PrioLens: stripping a pair down until only a minimal mechanical difference remains can make the pair locally clean while weakening the broader target meanings.

Therefore:
- do not render R02R;
- do not repair R02R by another tiny mechanical edit;
- relax the matched-edge requirement from `near-identical mechanism` to `shared context / balanced legitimacy / controlled salience`, allowing different actions when needed to preserve the constructs;
- the next MAS↔INF concept should make MAS visibly about quality of execution and INF visibly about consequence on a genuinely shared/external situation, while balancing actor status, effort, attractiveness and visual magnitude rather than forcing the same control mechanism.

This result does not invalidate INF cross-edge identity. R01 and R03 remain the two currently supported INF realizations. It shows that MAS↔INF needs a different operational grammar.
