# PrioLens architecture reassessment after Gates D1-D2 v0.1

Status: ARCHITECTURE GATE / NO NEW ASSETS
Date: 2026-08-31

## Trigger

Two deliberately selected cross-edge identity tests have not established safe direction-level aggregation:

- INF Gate D1: NOT PASSED / PARTIAL_IDENTITY / HOLD.
- MAS Gate D2: Claude = PARTIAL_IDENTITY; Grok = LOCAL_ONLY.

MAS was selected specifically because the operationalizability audit rated it the strongest current static-image direction. Its failure therefore matters more than a weak edge failure.

This is still AI concept-review evidence, not human construct validation. It is sufficient to stop assuming that broad labels can be safely aggregated before human evidence.

## What survived

The local contrasts themselves are often readable.

Examples:
- refine form vs preserve form;
- align/fix vs reveal current state;
- reciprocal exchange vs independent precision;
- redirect shared/external course vs configure own local setup.

Reviewers can often describe these differences cleanly before labels are revealed.

What does not reliably survive is the next inference step:

`several different local mechanisms -> one stable broad direction label`

INF repeatedly collapsed toward steering/routing/control.
MAS repeatedly collapsed toward local precision/fit/refinement mechanisms.

## Architectural diagnosis

The current design asks static images to do two jobs at once:

1. make a concrete tradeoff visually legible;
2. carry the same higher-order psychological meaning across materially different scenes.

Current evidence suggests job 1 is much easier than job 2.

A broad direction label may be an abstraction imposed by the designer after the fact rather than a meaning reliably present in each stimulus.

## Decision

Do not proceed to a third broad-direction Gate D3 yet.

Do not render the current MAS D2 set as a direction-validation set.

Do not rescue the architecture by narrowing labels post hoc merely to fit the images.

Move to an architecture comparison with the broad-direction model treated as only one candidate.

## Candidate architectures

### A. Direction-first aggregation (current hypothesis)

`broad direction -> several scene mechanisms -> choices -> direction score`

Current status: NOT SUPPORTED ENOUGH TO FREEZE.

Failure mode: scene mechanisms retain local meanings and do not converge reliably before label reveal.

### B. Observable-tradeoff-first architecture

`controlled local contrast -> participant choice -> repeated observable choice pattern -> descriptive session mirror`

No requirement that every pair maps onto one of eight predeclared directions.

Examples of result language:
- `Šioje sesijoje dažniau rinkaisi keisti formą, o ne ją išsaugoti.`
- `Kai reikėjo rinktis tarp matyti būseną ir ją koreguoti, dažniau rinkaisi korekciją.`
- `Socialiniame kontekste dažniau rinkaisi abipusį apsikeitimą nei individualų tikslumą.`

These are examples of inference form, not validated statements.

Advantage: interpretation stays close to what the scene actually manipulated.
Risk: result can become a collection of unrelated micro-observations unless empirical structure emerges.

### C. Empirical-cluster architecture

Start from validated local observable tradeoffs, collect human choice + open semantic data in research, then test whether some tradeoffs cluster into recurring higher-order families.

`local contrasts -> human semantic evidence + repeated choice data -> empirical clusters -> provisional higher-order labels`

Broad labels are earned after data rather than assumed before stimulus design.

Advantage: preserves possibility of discovering something like Mastery/Influence without forcing it.
Risk: requires more research data before a polished public score model exists.

## Recommended path

Use B as the safe product/research baseline and C as the discovery path.

Do not use A as the default until a broad direction independently survives cross-context semantic evidence.

This preserves the product constitution:

`START -> image pair -> choose -> ... -> short mirror`

The public experience can remain 2-4 minutes, visual and playful. Methodological complexity remains behind the product.

## Immediate research implication

Next work should not be another broad-direction pair set.

Build a small **observable tradeoff taxonomy** from the local contrasts that already survived blind concept review. For each candidate tradeoff, define:
- exactly what visible property differs;
- what nuisance cues must be controlled;
- what interpretations are allowed;
- what broader interpretations are explicitly not yet allowed.

Then design a compact human semantic screen before any score architecture.

## Falsifiable future return to directions

A higher-order direction can return only if:
1. multiple materially different local contrasts are independently described with a common semantic core before labels;
2. that common core survives different opponents and scene families;
3. human data reproduce the cross-context identity;
4. the broad label explains data better than local mechanism labels alone.

Until then, broad directions remain research hypotheses, not scoring units.

## Product boundary remains unchanged

Do not turn this into a long questionnaire or per-choice explanation workflow.

The product ambition remains:

> **For Fun, but with Wisdom.**

Short, visual, low-reading, no mandatory writing, session-bound interpretation, no diagnosis or hidden-self claims.
