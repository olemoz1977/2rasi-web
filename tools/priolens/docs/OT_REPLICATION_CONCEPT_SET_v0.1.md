# PrioLens observable tradeoff replication concept set v0.1

Status: PRE-RENDER / TEXT-LEVEL REPLICATION TEST
Date: 2026-08-31

## Purpose

Test whether four observable tradeoffs survive across three materially different scene families each before any rendering or human study.

This is not a psychological-construct test. The unit under test is a visible local relation.

Target families:
- OT-01 · OBSERVE STATE ↔ CHANGE STATE
- OT-02 · LOCAL CONFIGURATION ↔ SHARED-PROCESS STEERING
- OT-03 · PRESERVE FORM/STATE ↔ REFINE FORM/QUALITY
- OT-05 · PRECISION POSITIONING ↔ ROUTE STEERING

Each family has three scene realizations. A/B polarity is deliberately varied in the blind package.

## Design constraints across all pairs

- no text, labels, numbers or branded objects;
- neutral lighting and ordinary materials;
- no luxury, danger, urgency, success or failure cue;
- no obvious winner in usefulness, status, difficulty or emotional valence;
- no finished reward state;
- same actor count within a pair;
- same camera distance and approximate visual density within a pair;
- actions should be legible from one static frame;
- the contrast must not require a hidden backstory.

# OT-01 · OBSERVE STATE ↔ CHANGE STATE

Core relation:
- Observe = make the current state/relation directly visible without altering it.
- Change = physically alter the current state/relation/configuration without adding a reward or visible downstream success.

## OT01-R1 · Mechanical aperture panel

Shared scene:
A plain tabletop panel contains three identical circular discs mounted in a row inside a shallow neutral frame. One hand interacts with the center disc. Same materials, hand position zone, camera and lighting.

Observe variant:
The hand slides aside a small frosted inspection cover over the center disc, exposing the exact current orientation of its opening relative to the two neighboring openings. The discs themselves do not move.

Change variant:
The inspection cover is already open. The hand rotates the center disc a modest amount, visibly changing its opening orientation relative to the neighboring openings. No rod, flow, completion cue or downstream consequence is shown.

Nuisance controls:
- no visible success state after rotation;
- no gauge or specialist tool;
- both actions use one small hand movement in the same area.

## OT01-R2 · Modular storage tray

Shared scene:
A shallow neutral tray contains four identical plain blocks in a simple grid under one sliding translucent lid section. One hand interacts with the same central area. Same camera, lighting and object count.

Observe variant:
The hand slides the translucent lid section aside so the exact current arrangement of the four blocks becomes directly visible. The blocks remain untouched.

Change variant:
The lid section is already open. The hand slides one central divider laterally, changing the spatial arrangement available to the same four blocks. The blocks themselves are not removed, added or improved.

Nuisance controls:
- no neat-versus-messy framing;
- no “better organization” endpoint;
- the changed divider position is merely different, not visibly superior.

## OT01-R3 · Adjustable lamp head

Shared scene:
A plain work lamp with a spherical joint stands above a neutral matte tabletop. The lamp is off, so brightness/reward cannot differ. One hand interacts with the joint housing. Same framing and lighting.

Observe variant:
The hand opens a small plain inspection flap on the joint housing, revealing the current internal joint orientation through a clear window. The lamp head does not move.

Change variant:
The inspection flap is already open. The hand rotates the lamp head slightly at the same joint, visibly changing its orientation. The lamp remains off and no target object is illuminated.

Nuisance controls:
- no functional payoff from the new angle;
- no hidden mystery content;
- same device and hand location.

# OT-02 · LOCAL CONFIGURATION ↔ SHARED-PROCESS STEERING

Core relation:
- Local configuration = actor changes only the setup/method of their own bounded station.
- Shared-process steering = actor changes the route/allocation of an ongoing process beyond that local station.

The two sides must avoid authority/status cues and obvious difference in consequence magnitude.

## OT02-R1 · Tabletop bead-work system

Shared scene:
One adult works at a plain tabletop system where identical neutral beads arrive slowly along a central track and then pass by the actor's small local work zone. Two equal downstream tracks continue beyond the station. Same actor, camera, bead count and lighting.

Local-configuration variant:
The hand rotates a small reversible guide inside the actor's own shallow work tray, changing how beads are positioned within that local tray. The central and downstream tracks remain unchanged.

Shared-steering variant:
The same hand rotates an equally simple fork just after the local tray, changing which of two visually equal downstream tracks the next beads follow. Neither route is shorter, brighter, fuller or associated with another person.

Nuisance controls:
- identical control size and movement;
- equal downstream routes;
- no ownership symbols or authority cues.

## OT02-R2 · Water-channel bench model

Shared scene:
A neutral demonstration bench carries a shallow stream of clear water through one central channel past a single person's station, then toward two equal downstream channels. No plants, scarcity, damage or benefit cues. Same camera and lighting.

Local-configuration variant:
The hand adjusts a small baffle only inside the person's own shallow side basin, changing the local circulation pattern there while the main channel continues unchanged.

Shared-steering variant:
The hand adjusts an equally simple splitter immediately downstream, changing which of two equal channels receives the main flow. Both channels are empty, equivalent and equally visible.

Nuisance controls:
- no resource beneficiary;
- no “saving” or “wasting” water;
- equal physical action and control complexity.

## OT02-R3 · Neutral paper-feed workspace

Shared scene:
A plain manual paper-feed setup carries identical blank cards along a central tabletop path through one person's station and then into two equal open receiving lanes. No labels, productivity counters or other workers. Same card count and framing.

Local-configuration variant:
The hand repositions a reversible guide inside the person's own small working area, changing the orientation in which each blank card sits at that station. The incoming and outgoing paths remain unchanged.

Shared-steering variant:
The hand moves an equally simple diverter just beyond the work area, changing which of the two equal receiving lanes the next blank cards enter. Both lanes are identical and equally empty.

Nuisance controls:
- no faster/slower or good/bad route;
- no managerial context;
- no visible production gain.

# OT-03 · PRESERVE FORM/STATE ↔ REFINE FORM/QUALITY

Core relation:
- Preserve = actively stabilize an existing acceptable state against unwanted deformation/change.
- Refine = actively alter the same kind of object toward a more controlled form/fit/quality criterion.

Preservation actions are designed to require comparable care and precision so “skill” does not belong only to refinement.

## OT03-R1 · Soft-clay cylinder

Shared scene:
A plain ceramics bench with one undecorated soft-clay cylindrical vessel. Same worker shown from torso/hands, same clothing, camera, lighting and vessel state.

Preserve variant:
The worker carefully positions and secures the final piece of a three-part fitted padded cradle around the soft cylinder so its existing straight walls are held against deformation. Correct support placement visibly requires alignment, but the vessel's form is not changed.

Refine variant:
The vessel sits on a simple slow hand turntable. The worker carefully adjusts the same cylinder's wall and rim so the form becomes more even and controlled. No decorative finish or perfect final object is shown.

Nuisance controls:
- both actions require careful hand placement;
- no damage or rescue cue;
- no completed-product reward.

## OT03-R2 · Thin wax panel

Shared scene:
A neutral bench holds one plain thin rectangular wax panel, already intact and approximately flat. Same worker, hand visibility, lighting and camera. No artistic design or valuable object cues.

Preserve variant:
The worker carefully fits two plain edge supports and a shallow backing plate around the panel so its existing shape will remain stable. The supports must sit flush along the edges; the wax itself is not reshaped.

Refine variant:
The worker carefully uses a broad plain smoothing block to correct a small uneven area so the panel becomes more uniformly flat. No glossy finish or dramatic before/after difference is shown.

Nuisance controls:
- both require precise contact with the same fragile material;
- no specialist prestige tool;
- equal visual complexity.

## OT03-R3 · Wooden frame corner

Shared scene:
A simple unfinished square wooden frame lies on a neutral workbench. One corner is intact but not yet fixed permanently. Same worker, hands, tools, camera and lighting.

Preserve variant:
The worker carefully seats a fitted temporary corner brace around the existing right-angle corner and tightens one plain hand screw so the current geometry is held stable. The corner is not moved toward a new angle.

Refine variant:
The worker uses the same style of hand screw on a plain adjustable corner fixture to make a small correction so the joint becomes more exactly square. No finished frame or aesthetic reward is shown.

Nuisance controls:
- both use similar hardware and precision;
- both are active technical actions;
- only preserve-versus-correct relation differs.

# OT-05 · PRECISION POSITIONING ↔ ROUTE STEERING

Core relation:
- Precision positioning = make a bounded component sit at an exact local position/alignment.
- Route steering = change which equivalent path an existing moving element/flow follows.

The route side must not look more consequential; the precision side must not look more difficult or technical.

## OT05-R1 · Marble-track module

Shared scene:
A plain tabletop marble track carries identical neutral balls slowly toward a small central module. One hand interacts with one simple control. Two visually equal outgoing tracks are present. Same camera, lighting and ball count.

Precision-positioning variant:
The hand slides a short central rail piece a few millimeters until its two ends sit flush with adjacent fixed rail segments. No ball is shown passing through and no completion signal appears.

Route-steering variant:
The hand rotates an equally simple fork at the same module, changing which of two equal outgoing tracks the next ball would follow. No ball reaches an endpoint and neither route is visually favored.

Nuisance controls:
- same module size and hand movement magnitude;
- no visible reward after either action;
- equal route lengths and appearance.

## OT05-R2 · Flexible tube board

Shared scene:
A neutral board holds one clear flexible tube carrying a small sequence of identical neutral beads. The tube reaches a simple junction area with two equal continuation paths. One hand interacts with the junction. Same framing and lighting.

Precision-positioning variant:
The hand seats a short connector section into a plain alignment cradle so the connector sits exactly centered between two fixed tube ends. The beads remain upstream and no passage is shown.

Route-steering variant:
The hand rotates an equally plain junction selector so the same tube connects to one of two equal continuation paths. The beads remain upstream and no destination is shown.

Nuisance controls:
- no leakage, blockage or repair narrative;
- same local device complexity;
- route selection has no beneficiary or payoff.

## OT05-R3 · Card-slot mechanism

Shared scene:
A plain tabletop mechanism moves identical blank cards toward one central module with two equal outgoing slots. One hand operates the module. Same card count, lighting, camera and material palette.

Precision-positioning variant:
The hand adjusts a small guide plate so its edge sits exactly flush with two fixed reference edges inside the central module. No card is shown moving through it.

Route-steering variant:
The hand moves an equally simple neutral flap that directs the next blank card toward one of the two equal outgoing slots. Neither slot contains anything and no downstream result is visible.

Nuisance controls:
- no text or sorting meaning;
- no productivity signal;
- same movement scale and visual density.

## Text-level pass criterion

A family is promising only if a blind reviewer can group all or most of its three realizations around substantially the same visible relation without being given the family label.

Strong signal:
- 3/3 grouped together or described with near-equivalent relation language;
- no competing grouping by domain/material/action trope explains the set better.

Weak signal:
- only 2/3 align;
- grouping depends on generic active/passive, technical/nontechnical, useful/useless or similar nuisance dimensions.

Fail signal:
- scenes cluster primarily by domain or local mechanism;
- reviewer cannot recover recurring relations across scene families.

AI review is only concept screening. Human blind semantic replication remains required before public interpretation.
