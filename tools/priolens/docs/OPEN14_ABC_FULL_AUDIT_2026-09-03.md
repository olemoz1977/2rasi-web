## Purpose

Complete a design-level A/B/C challenge across all 14 families before runtime conversion.

This is not participant evidence and does not validate constructs. It asks whether the proposed three exemplars are sufficiently readable, non-duplicative and diverse to justify a no-repeat formative bank.

## Criteria

1. intended action/meaning readable in roughly one second;
2. no obvious exact or near-semantic duplicate that makes cross-exemplar evidence trivial;
3. neighbouring-family cross-load is tolerable and documented;
4. nuisance salience does not dominate the intended signal;
5. family has useful mechanism/context diversity;
6. D search only when a current A/B clearly loses or the proposed C itself is inadequate.
