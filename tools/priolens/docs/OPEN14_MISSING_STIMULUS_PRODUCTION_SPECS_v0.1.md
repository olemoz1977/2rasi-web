# PrioLens Open14 missing stimulus production specs v0.1

Status: READY FOR EXPLICIT IMAGE-CREATION REQUEST / TEXT ONLY
Date: 2026-09-01

## Purpose

Turn the four human-accepted mechanisms into production-ready scene specifications without generating any image.

These are **not validation claims**. They are constraints for creating vivid, quickly readable prototype scenes.

Global rules:
- one standalone image;
- one dominant action/event;
- understandable at small mobile size within about one second;
- photorealistic / ordinary-life visual language consistent with the current bank;
- neutral natural lighting and plausible color;
- no text, labels, symbols, diagrams, captions, awards or branded objects;
- do not sterilize emotion;
- avoid theatrical posing;
- preserve ordinary human warmth where the mechanism requires it.

## CARE-A — modest self-sacrifice

### Scene
Two ordinary adults walking or standing together in mild rain under one umbrella. The umbrella holder has deliberately shifted most of the cover toward the other person. The other person is visibly sheltered while the holder's outer shoulder / sleeve is clearly getting wet.

### Required visual evidence
- only one umbrella;
- cover asymmetry must be obvious;
- the helper's small personal cost must be visible in-frame;
- both people remain ordinary peers;
- no request gesture.

### First-read target
`pasirūpinti kitu savo komforto sąskaita`

### Reject if first read becomes
- romance;
- dramatic rescue;
- generic rain protection;
- both equally sheltered;
- staged heroism.

### Composition preference
Medium-close framing, enough to see umbrella geometry and the exposed wet shoulder without needing full-body context.

## CARE-B — proactive help without request

### Scene
One ordinary adult is standing or crouching awkwardly with one occupied hand / bag while several simple everyday items have fallen nearby. A second nearby adult has already begun picking up the dropped items. The person with the problem is still occupied and is not visibly asking for help.

### Required visual evidence
- asymmetric roles;
- helper acts first;
- no pointing, pleading or request gesture;
- 3–5 simple dropped items maximum;
- helper's action must dominate over general social interaction.

### First-read target
`pastebėti ir padėti neprašytam`

### Reject if first read becomes
- teamwork;
- both people jointly tidying;
- accident/emergency;
- flirtation;
- clutter / searching for lost objects.

### Composition preference
Medium framing with helper's hand clearly reaching for a dropped object while the other person is still occupied.

## RECOGNITION-A — restrained peer acknowledgement

### Scene
A small group of ordinary visually equal peers directs modest positive acknowledgement toward one person. Several people clap lightly, smile naturally, and one or two people show a clear thumbs-up gesture toward the focal person.

### Required visual evidence
- attention converges on one ordinary peer;
- restrained applause;
- natural smiles;
- one or two thumbs-up gestures;
- no prize, stage or hierarchy.

### First-read target
`būti pastebėtam ir įvertintam kitų`

### Reject if first read becomes
- party / celebration;
- winning competition;
- formal award ceremony;
- celebrity / leader admiration;
- generic happy group.

### Composition preference
4–6 people, focal person within the same physical level and ordinary dress as others. Group acknowledgement must be directional but not exaggerated.

## RECOGNITION-B — judgement sought / valued view

Status: BOUNDARY PROBE.

### Scene
A small group of visually equal peers is looking at one simple neutral plan, object or everyday situation. One peer holds/presents the neutral material toward a focal person while two or more others visibly turn and wait for that person's view. The focal person is receiving attention, not commanding, teaching or demonstrating.

### Required visual evidence
- group attention converges on one person's judgement;
- material is neutral and visually secondary;
- no head-of-table position;
- no pointing orders / commanding posture;
- no specialist equipment that makes expertise itself the dominant read.

### First-read target
`mano nuomonė / kompetencija čia vertinama`

### Reject if first read becomes
- manager leading a meeting;
- teacher explaining;
- expert demonstrating skill;
- generic conversation;
- presentation / introduction;
- hierarchy/status symbolism.

### Composition preference
3–5 peers around a neutral shared surface/object. The focal person should be visually equal in clothing, posture and physical height; asymmetry comes only from others seeking their view.

## Production acceptance check

Each generated prototype is reviewed independently using only:
1. What is the first spontaneous read in ~1 second?
2. Is the intended action/event actually visible without hidden story?
3. Is a competing interpretation more likely to dominate?
4. Does it remain clear at small mobile size?
5. Is it vivid enough to compete with the existing Open14 bank?

Possible decisions:
- KEEP;
- HOLD;
- DROP;
- REASSIGN;
- NEW-FAMILY.

Do not tweak indefinitely to rescue a failed mechanism. If the first-read problem persists, change the scene mechanism or drop the family exemplar.
