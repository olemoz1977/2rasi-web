# MAS↔INF shared-context concept R02S v0.1

Status: TEXT-ONLY RESEARCH CANDIDATE / NOT RENDER READY
Date: 2026-08-30

## Design correction

R02R showed that exact-mechanism matching can remove the constructs themselves.

New rule:
- target-construct asymmetry is allowed and required;
- nuisance asymmetry is not.

Therefore MAS may visibly involve skill/quality of execution and INF may visibly involve consequence on an external/shared situation. What must be controlled are unrelated advantages such as prestige, dramatic scale, completion, reward, status, threat, attractiveness or sheer object count.

## R02S · Same greenhouse, different action meaning

Shared context:
- ordinary neutral greenhouse/work area;
- same worker, clothing and body visibility;
- same camera distance and angle;
- same two ordinary planting beds visible;
- same plant density, lighting and color treatment;
- no lush harvest, flowers, food reward, danger, logos or text;
- both actions are ordinary legitimate work activities;
- no supervisor / follower roles.

Variant A candidate (hidden MAS):
- worker uses both hands to perform one clearly careful grafting / precise pruning-and-joining action on one ordinary plant stem;
- visible hand placement and alignment make execution quality relevant;
- no expensive specialist instrument, no gauge, no trophy-like finished result;
- the other bed remains present and unchanged;
- the image emphasis is controlled skilled execution on one bounded task.

Variant B candidate (hidden INF):
- same worker uses both hands on one ordinary central irrigation gate/manifold in the same greenhouse;
- moving the control visibly redirects the same modest water flow from one of the two equal beds to the other;
- total water amount, number of beds and plant attractiveness remain unchanged;
- no bed becomes greener, healthier, fuller or more rewarded;
- the visible consequence is outside the worker's immediate hand action: which external bed receives the shared flow changes.

## Intended contrast

A: quality/skill of execution on a bounded task.

B: meaningful effect on the direction/outcome of an external system.

## Main falsification risks

- A may look more expert/prestigious simply because grafting is specialized.
- B may still collapse to generic irrigation/routing.
- moving water may be more visually salient than a careful hand action.
- different action types may create aesthetic preference unrelated to the intended contrast.

If blind review describes A primarily as `skilled/precise execution` and B primarily as `changing where an external shared resource goes`, with no strong good/bad or salience dominance, R02S earns rendered testing.

If it collapses to `interesting craft vs boring valve`, `hard task vs easy task`, or `plant care vs irrigation`, stop iterating MAS↔INF for Gate D1 and replace the opponent.
