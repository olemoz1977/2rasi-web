## Stage 2 challenge

Do not rewrite your Stage 1 conclusions to fit this mapping.

For every M01–M14:

1. Does your blind **dominant contrast** correspond to the intended direction edge?
2. Does Variant A map more strongly to its intended side than to the opposing direction or another working direction?
3. Does Variant B map more strongly to its intended side than to the opposing direction or another working direction?
4. Does the pair actually create a **tradeoff between the two intended priorities**, or merely two different scene features?
5. Is the mapping visible without researcher-only story?
6. Does equal legitimacy survive after the intended meanings are known?
7. Classify the pair exactly one:
   - `SUPPORTED`
   - `CROSS_LOAD`
   - `INSUFFICIENT`
   - `NONE`
8. State the single strongest reason for that classification.

Then summarize by edge:

- AUT ↔ OPP
- AUT ↔ EXP
- AUT ↔ INF
- CER ↔ PRO
- EXP ↔ OPP
- MAS ↔ INF
- CON ↔ INF

For each edge answer:
- Does at least one of its two independent scene concepts survive?
- Do both concepts express the **same intended tradeoff through different scenes**, or are they actually testing different things?
- Is this edge more promising under matched-pair architecture than under unrelated global exemplars?

Final architecture verdict:

Choose one:
- `MATCHED_EDGE_PROMISING`
- `GLOBAL_EXEMPLAR_POOL_PREFERRED`
- `BOTH_REQUIRE_REDESIGN`
- `INSUFFICIENT_TO_CHOOSE`

Do not average scores. Preserve specific failure modes.
