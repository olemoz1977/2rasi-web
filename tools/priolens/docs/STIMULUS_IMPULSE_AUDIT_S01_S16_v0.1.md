# PrioLens stimulus impulse audit — S01–S16 v0.1

Status: ACTIVE AUDIT
Updated: 2026-08-31

## Rule

Audit each image before looking at its intended direction.

Order:

`image -> first spontaneous impulse/read -> secondary impulses -> only then possible direction fit -> KEEP / HOLD / DROP / REASSIGN`

Historical PASS/HOLD/DROP is evidence, not a constraint. A prior DROP may still be a useful image if it was simply assigned to the wrong construct.

## Immediate consequences

1. Do not preserve old direction assignments automatically.
2. S06 is primarily completion/order, not automatically certainty.
3. S10 is primarily containment/order, not automatically certainty.
4. S05 may be more useful as a choice/agency cue than as its historical label suggested.
5. S09 may be worth recovering as discovery even though it was previously rejected under a broader interpretation.
6. S13 remains useful as passive safeguarding even if it is weaker as active protection.
