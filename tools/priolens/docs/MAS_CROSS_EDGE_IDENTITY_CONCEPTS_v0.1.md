# PrioLens Gate D2 · MAS cross-edge identity concepts v0.1

Status: TEXT-ONLY CONCEPT GATE / PRE-RENDER / NOT CONSTRUCT VALIDATION
Date: 2026-08-31

## Purpose

Test whether MAS preserves one coherent observable meaning across materially different opponents and scene mechanisms before any images are rendered.

Working MAS definition:

> developing or applying competence, precision, skill or quality.

Gate D2 is an architecture falsification test. It does not validate Mastery as a stable trait.

## Why these three opponents

Use:
- CER · Certainty
- PRO · Protection
- CON · Connection

Do not use INF: MAS↔INF has already been exhausted during Gate D1.

The three selected opponents force MAS to survive across:
1. mechanical state/information context;
2. material-form/preservation context;
3. social context with people-count controlled.

If the recurring MAS meaning is only `careful hands`, `dexterity`, `craft work`, `technical tools`, or `professional skill`, Gate D2 is not passed at direction level.

## Cross-pair non-negotiables

- No text, labels, arrows, scores or success symbols.
- No luxury tools, uniforms, professional status or trophy-like finished result.
- No side should be brighter, safer, more complete, more rewarding or more visually dramatic.
- MAS must be carried by visible quality/precision of execution, not by expensive/specialist equipment.
- Opponent meaning must remain visible enough that the pair is not merely `skilled action vs doing nothing`.
- MAS scenes must use materially different mechanisms.

## M1 · MAS ↔ CER

Mechanism: **fine alignment vs state visibility**

### Shared scene

A neutral tabletop rail apparatus on a plain bench. Three identical sliding blocks contain circular apertures along one straight physical path. One ordinary hand interacts with the same central area in both variants. Same camera, lighting, materials, block count and overall visual density. No numbers, measurement marks, text or colored indicators.

### MAS candidate

A plain neutral rod already passes through the first aperture and approaches the second. The hand makes a small positional adjustment to the middle block so the apertures physically line up and the rod can continue smoothly through the three-block path. The visible issue is exact alignment and controlled fit. No gauge, caliper, specialist tool or emphasized finished endpoint.

### CER candidate

The blocks and rod remain physically unchanged. The hand slides aside one modest frosted inspection cover over the central section so the exact current positions and relation of all three apertures become directly visible through a clear window. Nothing is aligned, redirected or repaired. The reveal is complete enough to establish the current state, not staged as mystery or discovery.

### Main risks

- MAS may collapse to generic adjustment/problem solving.
- CER may collapse to inspection/reveal.
- `act vs inspect` may explain the pair more simply than MAS vs CER.

## M2 · MAS ↔ PRO

Mechanism: **form refinement vs preservation**

### Shared scene

A plain ceramics/work bench with one undecorated soft-clay cylindrical vessel. Same worker shown from torso/hands, same neutral clothing, same medium camera distance, same lighting, same vessel size and same muted background. No finished decorative pottery, branding, expensive tools, food or display context.

### MAS candidate

The vessel sits on a simple slow hand-operated turntable. Both hands refine the wall and rim so the form becomes more even and controlled. The quality of hand pressure and symmetry is visibly relevant. The vessel remains unfinished and undecorated; no perfect final result is highlighted.

### PRO candidate

The same soft-clay vessel is supported in a plain three-piece padded cradle. The worker uses both hands to secure the final soft side support so the thin vessel is held against deformation during handling/drying. No damage, emergency, darkness or high-value cue is present. The vessel is not being reshaped or improved in form.

### Main risks

- MAS may gain craft/aesthetic attraction.
- PRO may read as packing/maintenance rather than protection.
- The slow turntable can make MAS more dynamic than PRO.

## M3 · MAS ↔ CON

Mechanism: **quality-focused independent execution vs reciprocal exchange**

### Shared scene

Two equal-status adults sit opposite each other at a neutral worktable. Same people, clothing, face visibility, posture range, lighting and camera in both variants. Each side of the table contains the same number of identical plain wooden pieces and one simple neutral fixture. No smiles, celebration, hierarchy, business symbols or audience.

### MAS candidate

Both participants independently work on identical small joints in their own identical fixtures. Each carefully aligns two plain pieces so the joint sits flush and square before fastening. Attention is on fit and execution quality. Neither person instructs, watches or leads the other.

### CON candidate

The same pieces remain unjoined. Both participants simultaneously exchange identical neutral pieces across the center of the table: each passes one and receives one. Their hand/body orientation is reciprocal and directed toward one another. No person leads, instructs or evaluates the other, and no joint is formed or quality-tested.

### Main risks

- CON may read as cooperation/task coordination rather than relationship.
- MAS may read as independent work rather than mastery.
- Reciprocal orientation can retain social-attention salience even with people-count matched.

## Cross-edge MAS pass signal

A blind reviewer should independently find a recurring idea across the three intended MAS variants close to:
- execution quality matters;
- controlled fit/form/alignment;
- careful application of skill toward a better-formed or more exact result;
- competence expressed through how the action is performed.

A weaker recurring idea is not enough if it is mainly:
- using hands carefully;
- doing a craft;
- technical work;
- difficult work;
- working independently;
- manipulating objects precisely because the scene mechanically forces precision.
