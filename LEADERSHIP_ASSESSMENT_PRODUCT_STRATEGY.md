# Leadership Assessment — product strategy

Status: PRODUCT DIRECTION / RESEARCH-FIRST
Date: 2026-08-23

## Core decision

Build the first version as a **2rasi research / for-fun experience** with a deliberate path toward a serious professional assessment product later.

The first public versions are **not hiring or promotion instruments**.

Long-term professional use is conditional on job analysis, SME calibration, reliability, criterion validity, fairness review, item security, governance and documented human decision-making.

---

## Product split

### 2rasi.com — LAB / discovery / self-insight

Purpose:
- experiment with WorkStyle and Leadership SJT architecture;
- collect usability and research data;
- test wording, scenarios, visualisations and result explanations;
- run opt-in pilots;
- keep the experience engaging enough that people actually complete it;
- learn before making high-stakes claims.

Allowed claims:
- research prototype;
- self-reflection / development;
- situational judgement exploration;
- experimental result language;
- no selection recommendation.

Not allowed:
- suitable / unsuitable for management;
- leadership potential percentage;
- validated hiring assessment;
- automated promotion recommendation;
- hard competency cut-offs.

### omesg360.eu — PROFESSIONAL / organisational assessment

Future purpose only after evidence gates are met:
- leadership assessment for organisations;
- role profiles;
- structured assessment batteries;
- 360 integration;
- organisation-specific reporting;
- governance, security and candidate administration.

Do not duplicate the measurement engine. The long-term intention is one research lineage and one evidence base, with different product surfaces.

---

## Research-first architecture

Working stack:

`Role context -> WorkStyle -> Leadership SJT -> structured evidence / work sample -> interpretation`

Important separation:

- **WorkStyle** = behavioural preference / tendency.
- **Leadership SJT** = judgement in a concrete managerial situation.
- **Competence evidence** = behaviour demonstrated in interview, work sample, simulation or performance data.
- **Role fit** = match between validated evidence and analysed role requirements.

Preference is not competence. Competence is not role fit.

PAEI and DISC remain optional interpretation lenses. They are not selection keys.

---

## V1 target population

Research V1 should focus on a narrow family:

> Front-line manager with formal people responsibility for a co-located, task- or shift-based team performing largely standardised work and reporting to a more senior manager.

Examples:
- shift supervisor;
- production supervisor;
- warehouse / logistics team leader;
- service team leader.

Exclude from V1:
- project leads without formal people authority;
- senior strategic leaders;
- supervisors of highly autonomous expert professionals.

Those may become future role modules.

---

## Fun now, serious later

### Phase A — engaging research prototype

Optimise for:
- completion;
- understandable language;
- realistic situations;
- interesting feedback;
- mobile usability;
- transparent research positioning.

Do not optimise for:
- commercial-looking precision;
- percentile theatre;
- fake competency certainty;
- selection cut-offs.

### Phase B — research instrument

Add:
- job-analysis evidence;
- critical incidents;
- independent SME scoring;
- controlled item bank;
- psychometric pilots;
- language / fairness checks;
- criterion data where possible.

### Phase C — professional beta

Only after evidence supports it:
- organisation pilots;
- role-specific modules;
- structured interview / work-sample integration;
- human-reviewed reports;
- governance and audit trail.

### Phase D — monetised professional product

Possible monetisation:
- per-candidate assessment;
- organisation licence;
- leadership-development package;
- 360 + SJT + WorkStyle bundle;
- role-profile / assessment-centre services.

Pricing and commercial packaging are intentionally deferred until there is evidence worth selling.

---

## Evidence-preservation rules from day one

Even the for-fun version should preserve future usefulness.

1. Version every form and item bank.
2. Store item IDs independently of displayed order.
3. Store response latency only as research metadata, never as a competence score.
4. Keep `cannot assess / no experience` distinct where used.
5. Do not overwrite old pilot data after wording changes.
6. Record form language and version.
7. Do not reuse public legacy SJT stems verbatim in any future high-stakes form.
8. Keep WorkStyle and SJT scores/data separable.
9. Do not hard-code PAEI/DISC mappings as validated relationships.
10. Keep research hypotheses separate from product decision rules.

## Immediate next step

Run a lightweight Critical Incident discovery phase before writing the new SJT scenario bank.

Because the current product is research-first, use two levels:

### Stage 0A — exploratory discovery

Goal: learn quickly.

Target:
- 6–10 experienced front-line managers / managers-of-managers;
- preferably more than one organisation or sector;
- 2–3 useful incidents per person.

Claims unlocked: none. This is qualitative discovery only.

### Stage 0B — formal job-analysis foundation

Before any selection-validity claim:
- expand toward 15–25 interviews;
- include multiple organisations;
- collect frequency / importance / criticality judgements;
- use independent SME linkage to the final content blueprint.

Stage 0A can feed Stage 0B, but does not replace it.
