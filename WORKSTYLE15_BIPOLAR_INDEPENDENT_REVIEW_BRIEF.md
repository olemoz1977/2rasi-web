# WorkStyle 15 — Independent Methodology Review Brief

Status: BLIND / CHALLENGE-ORIENTED REVIEW
Date: 2026-08-23

## Reviewer role

Act as an independent psychometric / organizational-assessment methodology reviewer.

Your task is **not** to improve the proposed model by default. Your task is to determine whether the proposed direction is conceptually and methodologically defensible, and to recommend rejection or redesign where appropriate.

Do not assume that the current WorkStyle architecture, the number 15, the five domains, the bipolar hypothesis, or future PAEI/DISC overlays should be preserved.

## Core review question

Is WorkStyle 15 better conceptualised as:

A. 15 unipolar tendencies;
B. 15 bipolar axes;
C. a hybrid of bipolar and unipolar constructs;
D. a substantially different structure?

Give a clear recommendation and confidence level.

## Required analysis

### 1. Bipolarity

For each of the current 15 constructs:
- decide whether a meaningful psychological/work-style opposite exists;
- distinguish a true opposite from merely “low amount of the trait”;
- flag pairs where both proposed poles can plausibly be high at the same time;
- flag pairs where one pole is socially desirable and the other is implicitly undesirable;
- identify cases where the proposed axis is actually two constructs forced into one scale.

Return a table with:
- construct;
- proposed pole pair;
- verdict: strong bipolar / possible bipolar / should remain unipolar / split or replace;
- rationale;
- suggested alternative if needed.

### 2. Response format

Evaluate the proposed two-anchor ordinal response format.

Specifically assess:
- 5 vs 7 response positions;
- whether hidden signed coding such as -2…+2 is defensible;
- whether a midpoint can legitimately represent a neutral/balanced position;
- whether `depends on context` should be separate from midpoint;
- whether `cannot assess / no experience` should remain missing data;
- risk of forced-choice-like distortion even though the format is not formally ipsative forced choice.

### 3. Item design

Assess whether three retained scenarios per axis can be sufficient for a reflective public tool, provided a larger candidate item bank is used during validation.

Recommend:
- initial candidate-item count per axis;
- cognitive-testing process;
- item-reduction criteria;
- how to prevent repeated scenario templates and semantic duplication;
- how to prevent the respondent from inferring a morally preferred answer.

### 4. Scoring

Evaluate alternatives to a simple arithmetic signed mean.

Discuss:
- ordinal treatment;
- polychoric correlation approaches;
- factor/ESEM/CFA approaches;
- whether IRT-type modelling is appropriate or unnecessarily complex at this stage;
- reliability estimation;
- missing-data handling;
- test–retest expectations;
- whether within-axis response dispersion has interpretable meaning with only three final items.

Explicitly assess this warning:

> A mean of zero from `-2, 0, +2` is not psychologically equivalent to `0, 0, 0`, but dispersion may reflect context sensitivity, ambiguity, multidimensionality or unreliability. Should it be retained only as a research diagnostic rather than a respondent-facing construct?

### 5. Domains

Evaluate the current five-domain grouping:
- Implementation / Įgyvendinimas
- Adaptation / Adaptacija
- Openness to Others / Atvirumas kitiems
- Influence / Poveikis
- Self-Regulation / Savireguliacija

Questions:
- Are these useful conceptual groupings under a bipolar architecture?
- Should they have numerical scores?
- Is averaging signed axes inside a domain theoretically meaningful?
- Should the domains be removed from scoring until a higher-order structure is empirically demonstrated?

### 6. DISC relationship

Independently assess whether signed bipolar WorkStyle data are suitable for a later DISC-style correspondence lens.

Do **not** assume a direct mapping.

Address:
- the difference between WorkStyle axes and a two-dimensional circumplex;
- whether WorkStyle axes could be projected empirically into an Agency/Communion-like or DiSC-like space;
- what benchmark data would be needed;
- what statistical approach would be defensible;
- whether a hand-designed D/i/S/C assignment should be rejected;
- how to avoid implying that WorkStyle reproduces or substitutes for an official DiSC assessment.

### 7. PAEI relationship

Independently assess whether signed bipolar WorkStyle data are suitable for later PAEI correspondence.

Address:
- whether Producer, Administrator, Entrepreneur and Integrator should be treated as four independent/overlapping projections rather than a zero-sum profile;
- whether any WorkStyle axes have plausible cross-loadings on multiple PAEI roles;
- what evidence is needed before numerical P/A/E/I projections are shown;
- whether a qualitative lens is safer than quantitative derived scores.

### 8. Visualization

Evaluate whether a classic radar chart is conceptually misleading for bipolar axes.

Recommend a visualization that:
- gives equal visual status to both poles;
- does not imply outward = better;
- can display 15 axes without excessive clutter;
- could later support an optional outer correspondence layer for PAEI or DISC without suggesting equivalence;
- does not require showing raw numbers to the user.

### 9. Validation design

Propose a realistic validation sequence for a small independent project.

Please separate:
- cognitive/item-development sample;
- initial structural pilot;
- larger structural/validation sample;
- test–retest sample;
- external convergent/discriminant validation;
- cross-validation/holdout sample if mappings are estimated.

Give practical sample-size ranges and explain what claims are and are not justified at each stage.

### 10. Failure criteria

Define explicit conditions under which the bipolar hypothesis should be rejected or restricted.

Examples to evaluate:
- proposed pole pairs correlate weakly rather than inversely;
- items from the two ends form separate factors;
- midpoint is dominated by ambiguity/context rather than balanced preference;
- high social desirability makes one pole systematically preferred;
- hybrid model fits substantially better;
- 15-axis structure does not replicate;
- PAEI/DISC mapping is unstable out of sample.

## Required final output format

Please return:

1. **Executive verdict** — choose A, B, C or D and confidence 0–100.
2. **15-axis bipolarity audit table**.
3. **Top 5 methodological strengths** of the proposal.
4. **Top 5 methodological risks/failures**.
5. **Recommended response format and scoring strategy**.
6. **Recommended validation plan**.
7. **DISC integration verdict** — feasible / feasible only as qualitative lens / not justified, with conditions.
8. **PAEI integration verdict** — feasible / feasible only as qualitative lens / not justified, with conditions.
9. **Visualization recommendation**.
10. **Go / revise / stop decision before writing the next item bank**.

Please distinguish clearly between:
- conclusions supported by established measurement theory;
- your own inference;
- points that require empirical data before they can be decided.

Do not optimize for agreement with the project owner or previous reviewers.
