# WorkStyle 15 — decision log

Status: ACTIVE WORKING DECISIONS

## Current product identity

Name: **WorkStyle 15**

Purpose: individual reflection on how a person tends to work.

Not a diagnostic personality test and not an official PAEI, DISC or MBTI assessment.

## Source/provenance finding

The recovered legacy model is not merely similar to a generic workplace-style framework. External verification shows that its **15 dimensions and 6 higher-level styles align essentially one-to-one with Aon's ADEPT-15 model**.

The correspondence is:
- Task style = Drive + Structure;
- Adaptation style = Conceptual + Flexibility + Mastery;
- Achievement style = Ambition + Power;
- Interaction style = Assertiveness + Liveliness;
- Emotional style = Composure + Positivity + Awareness;
- Teamwork style = Cooperation + Sensitivity + Humility.

This matches the Lithuanian legacy architecture:
- Kryptingumas + Struktūra;
- Konceptualus mąstymas + Lankstumas + Tobulėjimas;
- Ambicija + Įtaka;
- Asertiškumas + Gyvybingumas;
- Ramybė + Pozityvumas + Savimonė;
- Bendradarbiavimas + Jautrumas + Nuolankumas.

The project owner does not recall the exact origin of the 150 Lithuanian Likert statements and suspects they may have been generated by Microsoft Copilot from an international example. Treat that as plausible but **unverified** provenance.

Important distinction:
- Aon's official ADEPT-15 is an adaptive multidimensional forced-choice assessment, not a simple 150-item Likert questionnaire;
- the legacy Microsoft Forms tool therefore appears to be an unofficial Likert analogue built around the public ADEPT-15 construct architecture, not the official ADEPT-15 instrument.

Product consequence:
- original WorkStyle 15 item wording reduces dependence on unknown legacy item wording, but it does **not** erase the provenance of the 15-dimension / 6-style architecture;
- do not market WorkStyle 15 as an independently invented 15-dimension model unless the architecture is subsequently redesigned on an independent theoretical basis;
- before public launch, decide whether to (A) transparently describe the 15-dimension structure as inspired by established workplace-personality constructs / ADEPT-15 architecture, or (B) re-derive and rename the WorkStyle structure from broader public-domain constructs such as Five-Factor personality and independently selected workplace dimensions;
- never use the ADEPT-15 name, branding, proprietary item bank, adaptive scoring claims or imply equivalence to Aon's validated assessment.
