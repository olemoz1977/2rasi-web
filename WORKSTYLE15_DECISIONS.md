# WorkStyle 15 — decision log

Status: ACTIVE WORKING DECISIONS
Last updated: 2026-08-23

## Current product identity

Name: **WorkStyle 15**

Purpose: individual reflection on how a person tends to work.

Not a diagnostic personality test and not an official PAEI, DISC or MBTI assessment.

## Source/provenance finding

External verification shows that the recovered legacy model's **15 dimensions and 6 higher-level styles align essentially one-to-one with Aon's ADEPT-15 model**.

BPS describes ADEPT-15 as 15 traits grouped into 6 styles and states that 10 traits map to the Five Factor Model while 5 extend into workplace/leadership-related traits.

The legacy LT structure corresponds closely to:
- Task = Kryptingumas + Struktūra;
- Adaptation = Konceptualus mąstymas + Lankstumas + Tobulėjimas;
- Achievement = Ambicija + Įtaka;
- Interaction = Asertiškumas + Gyvybingumas;
- Emotional = Ramybė + Pozityvumas + Savimonė;
- Teamwork = Bendradarbiavimas + Jautrumas + Nuolankumas.

The project owner does not recall the exact origin of the 150 Lithuanian Likert statements and suspects they may have been generated by Microsoft Copilot from an international example. Treat that as plausible but **unverified** provenance.

Important distinction:
- Aon's official ADEPT-15 is an adaptive multidimensional forced-choice assessment, not the legacy 150-item Likert Forms implementation;
- the legacy tool should therefore be treated as an unofficial analogue, not as the official ADEPT-15 instrument.

## Architecture decision — independent WorkStyle 15

**Chosen direction: independently re-derived WorkStyle 15.**

Do not keep the inherited 6 ADEPT-style groups as the product's primary architecture.

Retain the useful breadth of 15 work tendencies, but reorganize them into a new 5-domain workplace-reflection model defined in `WORKSTYLE15_INDEPENDENT_MODEL_V01.md`:

1. **Execution**
   - Goal Persistence / Kryptingumas
   - Work Structure / Struktūra
   - Achievement Drive / Pasiekimų siekis

2. **Adaptation**
   - Systems Thinking / Sisteminis mąstymas
   - Adaptability / Prisitaikymas
   - Learning Orientation / Mokymosi orientacija

3. **Collaboration**
   - Cooperation / Bendradarbiavimas
   - Empathic Attention / Empatinis dėmesys
   - Intellectual Humility / Intelektinis nuolankumas

4. **Influence**
   - Assertive Communication / Asertyvus komunikavimas
   - Persuasive Influence / Įtaka
   - Social Energy / Socialinė energija

5. **Self-Regulation**
   - Emotional Regulation / Emocinė savitvarda
   - Constructive Outlook / Konstruktyvus požiūris
   - Reflective Self-Awareness / Reflektyvi savimonė

Reason:
- breaks the one-to-one inherited 6-style arrangement;
- anchors the model in broader personality/work-behaviour concepts rather than one commercial assessment architecture;
- produces clearer workplace language;
- keeps the useful 15-dimensional resolution for reflection and future PAEI/DISC lens work.

This is a **design hypothesis**, not psychometric validation.

The current draft in `WORKSTYLE15_ORIGINAL_75_LT_DRAFT.md` is retained as source material, but its item labels/wording must now be remapped to the independent 5×3 model before coding.

The previous 45-legacy + 30-new Beta A blueprint remains historical/fallback only.

## Preferred size and scoring

First pilot: **75 items = 15 dimensions × 5 items**.

Working scale:
- Likert 1–5;
- direct items score directly;
- reverse items score `6 - response`;
- dimension index remains a transparent theoretical scale index, not a percentile.

Do not shorten to 60 until pilot evidence supports removal.

## Result architecture

Primary result should become:
- 15 measured WorkStyle dimensions;
- 5 WorkStyle domains as summary layer;
- strengths/trade-offs and reflection prompts;
- no fixed type labels;
- no claim that high = good or low = weak.

Existing interpretation drafts must be updated from the old 6-style grouping to the new 5-domain model.

## External lenses

PAEI and DISC remain optional derived lenses.

Current rule:
- rebuild mappings against the revised 15 constructs;
- first beta may highlight conceptual correspondence only;
- no official/numeric PAEI or DISC claim before item-level mapping and pilot evidence;
- preferred UI remains one outer toggle `PAEI | DISC` over the same measured core.
