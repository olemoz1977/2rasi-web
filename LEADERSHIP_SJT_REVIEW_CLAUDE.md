# Leadership SJT — Independent Review (Claude)

Status: INDEPENDENT REVIEW / ADVERSARIAL
Date: 2026-08-23
Branch: hero-webgl

## Executive verdict

**C — SUBSTANTIAL COMPETENCY REDESIGN REQUIRED**

Confidence: **78 / 100**

Primary uncertainty: there is no formal job analysis, item-level data, respondent covariance data or criterion data yet.

The SJT method is considered defensible. The current 8+1 architecture is not.

## Central methodological warning

The current hypothesis risks conflating:

1. **content blueprint** — the domains and scenario families the item bank should cover;
2. **measurement model** — the number of psychometrically defensible scores the instrument can report.

Claude argues that an SJT can use several content domains without being able to report a reliable subscore for every domain. V1 should therefore plan for **one primary reported situational managerial judgement score**, with facet scores unlocked only if later empirical structure supports them.

Recommended label:

**Situational managerial judgement (SJT-MJ)**

Avoid:
- leadership potential %;
- x/80 effectiveness;
- suitable / unsuitable;
- automatic reporting of 8–9 competency subscores.

## Recommended V1 target role

> **Front-line manager with formal people responsibility for a co-located, task- or shift-based team performing largely standardised work, reporting to a middle manager, without budget or hiring authority.**

Includes manufacturing/production supervisors, warehouse/logistics team leaders and service team leaders.

Exclude from V1:
- project leads without formal authority;
- supervisors of expert professionals;
- senior/strategic leaders.

## Recommended five-domain content blueprint

### D1. Expectation and accountability management
Merge C2 Direction/clarity + C4 Performance accountability.

### D2. Capability development and autonomy calibration
Merge C3 Delegation + C5 Coaching/development. Retain delegation and coaching as different content families even if they do not become separate scores.

### D3. Interpersonal and conflict judgement
Refine C6. Escalation is removed from this domain.

### D4. Prioritisation and escalation judgement
Refine C8 and absorb escalation judgement.

### D5. Ethical, safety and fairness judgement
**New high-priority domain.** Covers safety shortcuts, near-miss reporting, record integrity, fair task/overtime allocation, compliance and equal treatment.

## Constructs dropped or reassigned

### C1 Situational diagnosis
**Drop as standalone competency.** Treat as:
- item-design requirement;
- optional response-process evidence;
- possible later rationale sub-test on selected scenarios.

Reason: diagnosis is upstream of nearly every good SJT response, so scoring it across most items risks creating a noisy copy of the total.

Additional concern: the current skill/clarity/confidence/motivation categories retain part of the Situational Leadership readiness ontology even after removing S1–S4 scoring.

### C7 Change and uncertainty leadership
**Drop as competency.** Treat change as a crossed scenario context: steady state vs active change.

### C9 Operational / systems judgement
**Move outside the common SJT.** Measure with role-specific job knowledge or work sample.

Also outside the SJT where appropriate:
- planning/resource allocation → in-basket work sample;
- communication clarity → work sample/interview;
- resilience/preferences/learning orientation → WorkStyle for development use;
- technical/trade competence → separate gate.

## Recommended response format

Operational candidate form:
- knowledge instruction: **Which action would be most effective?**
- most-effective / least-effective pair;
- 4–5 realistic managerial strategies per scenario;
- no S1–S4 mapping;
- no time limit; latency only for data-quality monitoring.

Calibration stage:
- SMEs and pilot participants rate all options for effectiveness on a 1–7 scale.

Optional later experiment:
- rationale/diagnosis probe on selected high-value scenarios.

## Recommended scoring architecture

- SME-consensus keying with partial credit.
- Items with genuine expert disagreement are revised/dropped rather than forced into a single author key.
- Multidimensional competency coding remains an internal **blueprint/coverage tool**, not proof of multiple reportable scales.
- V1 plans for one primary SJT-MJ score.
- Any 2–3 facet scores require later structural evidence and adequate facet reliability.
- No cut scores before criterion validation.
- No “leadership potential” claim.

## WorkStyle relationship

Keep WorkStyle and SJT:
- scored independently;
- reported independently;
- jointly interpreted only after validation.

Do not turn plausible combinations such as `autonomy preference + weak delegation judgement` into selection rules without incremental-validity and fairness evidence.

## Legacy bank implication

The 20 public legacy stems may be used as structural inspiration and scenario-family evidence, but **must not be reused verbatim in a future selection instrument**, because they are publicly accessible together with a live key.

## Validation sequence

1. Job analysis / Critical Incident Technique
2. Blueprint + item writing
3. Cognitive pretesting
4. Two independent SME calibration panels
5. N≈100 usability pilot
6. STOP/REDESIGN gate
7. N≈300–500 structural validation
8. Criterion validation
9. Monitored operational use

Claude proposes an explicit pilot gate including:
- enough surviving items;
- acceptable total-score precision;
- adequate item–total functioning;
- a check that the instrument is not merely a verbal-reasoning test in disguise;
- language/fairness checks.

Exact numeric thresholds proposed in the review are hypotheses to be verified against current methodological literature before being adopted as formal decision rules.

## Final decision

**REVISE before writing the new scenario bank.**

First:
- run Stage 0 job analysis;
- narrow the role;
- revise the competency blueprint;
- explicitly separate blueprint from measurement model;
- add ethical/safety/fairness content;
- move WorkStyle × SJT interaction ideas into a research register;
