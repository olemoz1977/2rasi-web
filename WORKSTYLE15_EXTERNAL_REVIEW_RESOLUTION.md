# WorkStyle 15 — independent review resolution

Status: REVIEW COMPLETE / ACTIONED FOR V0.2
Date: 2026-08-23

## Independent review outcome

Blind first-pass verdict: **READY AFTER MINOR REVISIONS**.
Reviewer confidence: **72/100**.

Second-pass provenance reveal: **verdict unchanged**.

The reviewer concluded that the current 5-domain architecture is sufficiently independent from the historical ADEPT-like starting point to proceed as its own pre-pilot instrument, provided provenance remains documented transparently.

Overall construct-overlap judgment: **Normal**.

Primary structural watch-outs for pilot:
- Social Energy vs Persuasive Influence vs Assertive Communication;
- Constructive Outlook vs Emotional Regulation;
- Achievement Drive vs Goal Persistence;
- Learning Orientation vs Reflective Self-Awareness.

Social Energy received the weakest distinctness judgment and remains the main structural hypothesis to test empirically. It is **not removed before pilot**.

## Resolution of the reviewer’s 10 priority actions

### 1. Rewrite EMP-05 · R
**Decision: ACCEPT.**

Reason: current wording is strongly socially undesirable and may suppress honest variance.

V0.2 wording:
> Kai spaudžia terminas, kitų emocinė reakcija man paprastai turi mažesnę reikšmę negu pats rezultatas.

### 2. Rewrite REF-05 · R
**Decision: ACCEPT.**

Reason: remove double-negative processing load.

V0.2 wording:
> Kai problema atsiranda, pirmiausia ieškau išorinių priežasčių, o ne savo indėlio į ją.

### 3. Differentiate LRN-01 from REF-03
**Decision: ACCEPT.**

Reason: LRN should describe task/process learning; REF should describe self-attribution and interpersonal impact.

V0.2 LRN-01:
> Po sudėtingos užduoties peržiūriu savo darbo eigą ir nusprendžiu, ką kitą kartą keisiu procese.

REF-03 remains focused on one’s own contribution to a difficult interaction.

### 4. Rewrite PER-01 and HUM-01 from ability claims
**Decision: ACCEPT.**

PER-01 v0.2:
> Kai keli darbai konkuruoja dėl mano dėmesio, atsisakau mažiau svarbių veiklų, kad išlaikyčiau dėmesį ties svarbiausiu.

HUM-01 v0.2:
> Kai kito žmogaus argumentai ar duomenys stipresni, pakeičiu savo poziciją net kitų akivaizdoje.

### 5. Flag Social Energy for structural review / ENG-04 concern
**Decision: DEFER STRUCTURAL DECISION TO PILOT; MINOR ITEM REWRITE.**

Reason: whether Social Energy is redundant with influence/assertiveness is empirical. Removing it before data would pre-decide the question the pilot is intended to answer.

ENG-04 is retained as an intentionally dispositional workplace-social anchor but narrowed to work context:
> Naujoje darbo grupėje paprastai gana greitai įsitraukiu į bendrą diskusiją.

Pilot must test whether Social Energy adds discriminant information beyond Persuasive Influence and Assertive Communication.

### 6. Differentiate REG-03 from OUT-03
**Decision: ACCEPT.**

REG-03 v0.2 focuses on emotional recovery:
> Po stiprios emocinės reakcijos į netikėtą problemą paprastai gana greitai atgaunu darbinę būseną.

OUT-03 remains focused on cognitive possibility / next-step orientation.

### 7. Add a second reverse item per dimension
**Decision: DO NOT APPLY BEFORE PILOT.**

Reason:
- the current 75-item design already includes 15 reverse items (20% of all items);
- reverse wording itself may create method effects and comprehension errors;
- do not force a numerical R/D ratio without evidence that acquiescence is a problem in this population.

Pilot should compare direct vs reverse behaviour and only add/change reverse items if response patterns justify it.

### 8. Add post-results specificity/Barnum feedback
**Decision: ACCEPT.**

Pilot should ask after results:
1. `Kiek šis aprašymas atrodė būdingas būtent jums?` — 1–5.
2. Optional open comment: `Kas atrodė labai tikslu, per daug bendra arba netikslu?`

### 9. Gate PAEI/DISC lenses behind optional extended view
**Decision: ACCEPT / ALREADY PRODUCT RULE.**

Default result hierarchy:
1. 15 measured WorkStyle dimensions;
2. 5 WorkStyle domains as summary;
3. optional `PAEI | DISC` correspondence view.

No numeric PAEI/DISC scores in first pilot.

### 10. Specify completion-time budget and measure abandonment
**Decision: ACCEPT.**

Pilot instrumentation should record:
- total completion time;
- item or block response latency where practical;
- abandonment point;
- unanswered/skipped items if the UI allows skipping;
- first 20 completed sessions should be used to inspect median response time before any shortening decision.

No fixed public completion-time claim should be made before observing real pilot use.

## Empirical questions for pilot

Do not settle these by further wording debate alone:
- Is Social Energy distinct from Persuasive Influence and Assertive Communication?
- Are Constructive Outlook and Emotional Regulation distinct?
- Are Achievement Drive and Goal Persistence distinct?
- Do Learning Orientation and Reflective Self-Awareness separate after the LRN-01 rewrite?
- Which items show ceiling/floor compression or weak variance?
- Do reverse items behave differently from direct items?
- Does any broad social-desirability / favourable-self-description factor dominate responses?
- Does the 75-item length create abandonment or fatigue?
- Do result interpretations feel specific rather than generic?

## Analysis caution

Small cognitive pilots are for comprehension, variance clues and UX, not formal validation.

Factor analysis, CFA/model comparison, bifactor method models or strong reliability/validity claims require a sufficiently large and appropriate sample. Do not present such analyses from a tiny first pilot as validation.
