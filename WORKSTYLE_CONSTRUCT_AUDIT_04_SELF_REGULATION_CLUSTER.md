# WorkStyle construct audit 04 — Emotional Regulation, Constructive Outlook, Reflective Self-Awareness

Status: CONSTRUCT AUDIT / PRE-ITEM-WRITING
Date: 2026-08-23
Branch: hero-webgl

## Purpose

Evaluate whether the current self-regulation cluster contains defensible WorkStyle constructs, whether any are genuinely bipolar, and whether they should survive into the reduced architecture.

Source material:

## 1. Emotional Regulation / Emocinė savitvarda (`REG`)

Current v0.6 items:
- `REG-01` Spaudimo metu pirmiausia susidėlioju faktus, o tik tada reaguoju.
- `REG-04` Kai gaunu kritiką, pirmiausia išklausau ją iki galo.
- `REG-05` Po netikėtos problemos man dažnai reikia laiko grįžti į įprastą darbo ritmą.

### Construct problem

The three items do not measure one clean work-style continuum.

They mix:
- response inhibition / deliberation under pressure;
- receptivity during criticism;
- recovery speed after disruption.

These are related to self-regulation but are not the same behaviour.

### Bipolarity problem

`Regulation ↔ emotional expression` is not a defensible axis.

A person can be:
- highly regulated and highly expressive;
- highly regulated and restrained;
- poorly regulated and expressive;
- poorly regulated and withdrawn.

Therefore emotional expression is not the opposite of regulation.

### WorkStyle vs competence problem

The current wording also carries a strong evaluative gradient. In most workplaces:
- listening before reacting;
- staying composed;
- recovering quickly

are read as desirable capacities rather than neutral work-style alternatives.

This makes `REG` a weak fit for the intended non-evaluative WorkStyle core.

### Decision

**DROP current Emotional Regulation from the WorkStyle core.**

Do not force a bipolar replacement.

Possible future research constructs, if needed later:
- emotional reactivity (unipolar temperament-like measure);
- contained ↔ open emotional expression (possible style axis).

Neither is adopted now because both would require a fresh construct definition and new item bank.

## 2. Constructive Outlook / Konstruktyvus požiūris (`OUT`)

Current v0.6 items:
- `OUT-02` Po nesėkmės gana greitai pereinu prie to, ką galima daryti toliau.
- `OUT-03` Kai ne viską galiu pakeisti, susitelkiu į tai, ką dar galiu paveikti.
- `OUT-05` Kai planas stringa, pirmiausia ilgiau analizuoju, kas nepavyko, o tik tada judu toliau.

### Current construct problem

The current name implies a desirable positive mindset.

The items mix:
- recovery speed;
- locus of control / action focus;
- analysis-before-action.

`OUT-05` was already cognitively problematic because careful analysis is not the opposite of constructive action.

### Salvageable work-style signal

There is a stronger neutral work-style construct hidden inside the current dimension:

**Opportunity scanning ↔ Risk / obstacle scanning**

This asks what a person tends to notice first when evaluating a plan, proposal, or uncertain situation.

Both poles can be useful:
- opportunity focus may accelerate action and experimentation;
- risk focus may prevent avoidable failures.

The construct must not be framed as optimism ↔ pessimism.

### Decision

**RETAIN only as a redesigned conditional bipolar candidate.**

Working construct:

`Opportunity scanning ↔ Risk scanning`

Status: CONDITIONAL BIPOLAR / requires pole review.

Do not carry the current OUT items into the next bank.

## 3. Reflective Self-Awareness / Reflektyvi savimonė (`REF`)

Current v0.6 items:
- `REF-01` Po sudėtingo pokalbio pagalvoju, ką galėjau padaryti kitaip.
- `REF-03` Kai panaši problema kartojasi, pasižiūriu, ar prie jos neprisideda mano įprastas elgesys.
- `REF-05` Kai problema jau išspręsta, dažniausiai prie savo vaidmens joje nebegrįžtu.

### Construct problem

Self-reflection has no clean legitimate opposite.

`Reflection ↔ external/action focus` is artificial because people can be high on both:
- reflect on their own contribution;
- analyse external causes;
- move into action.

### Measurement circularity

There is an additional problem: a self-report instrument is being used to estimate reflective self-awareness — effectively asking respondents to self-rate how accurately they reflect on themselves.

This creates a circularity risk and strong social-desirability pressure.

### WorkStyle fit

Reflection may be useful as a development topic, but it is closer to metacognitive habit / developmental capability than to a neutral work-style preference.

### Decision

**DROP REF as a scored WorkStyle construct.**

It may remain later as:
- an unscored reflection prompt;
- a coaching/development layer;
- a qualitative follow-up after results.

It should not occupy a core axis in the reduced measurement architecture.

---

## 4. Cluster decision

From the current three-dimension `Savireguliacija` domain:

| Current construct | Decision |
|---|---|
| Emotional Regulation | DROP from WorkStyle core |
| Constructive Outlook | REDESIGN as `Opportunity scanning ↔ Risk scanning` |
| Reflective Self-Awareness | DROP as scored construct |

This means the old `Savireguliacija` domain should **not** be preserved as a three-dimension numerical domain by inheritance.

The five-domain structure is therefore increasingly best treated as historical organisation, not a target measurement model.

---

## 5. Implication for the reduced architecture

This audit removes two dimensions without replacing them simply to preserve symmetry.

The remaining `OUT` signal becomes a neutral attentional/preference axis rather than a positive-trait score.

This is consistent with the emerging principle:

> Keep only constructs that describe a recurring work-style choice or attentional default. Do not keep a construct merely because it represents a desirable managerial capability.
