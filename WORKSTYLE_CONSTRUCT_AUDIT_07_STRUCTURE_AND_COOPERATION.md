# WorkStyle construct audit 07 — Work Structure and Cooperation

Status: CONSTRUCT AUDIT / PRE-ITEM-WRITING
Date: 2026-08-23
Branch: hero-webgl

## Purpose

Confirm whether `Work Structure` and `Cooperation` are sufficiently clear and genuinely bipolar to anchor the reduced WorkStyle architecture.

No new items are written here.

---

## 1. Work Structure / Struktūra (`ORG`)

Current v0.6 items:
- `ORG-01` Prieš darbo pradžią susidėlioju pirmus žingsnius.
- `ORG-04` Pasikartojantį darbą susitvarkau taip, kad kitą kartą būtų paprasčiau.
- `ORG-05` Kai darbų daug, dažniausiai viską laikau galvoje.

### Construct signal

The current dimension broadly targets whether a person tends to externalise and pre-structure work versus organise it more emergently while acting.

A strong neutral bipolar frame is available:

**Structured preparation ↔ Emergent / improvisational organisation**

Both poles can be functional:
- structure supports predictability, coordination and repeatability;
- improvisational organisation supports speed, flexibility and low overhead when the situation is changing or simple.

### Item-bank warning

The current reverse item `viską laikau galvoje` is not a clean opposite of structure. It mixes:
- internal vs external memory;
- documentation habits;
- workload complexity.

Therefore the construct survives, but the old items do not define the future bipolar bank.

### Boundary with Adaptability

Keep distinct:
- Structure = how much work is organised before / around execution;
- Adaptability = how readily the chosen method changes when conditions change.

A person can be highly structured and highly adaptive.

### Decision

**KEEP as a strong bipolar core axis.**

Working pole pair:

`Structured preparation ↔ Emergent organisation`

Status: STRONG BIPOLAR.

---

## 2. Cooperation / Bendradarbiavimas (`COP`)

Current v0.6 items:
- `COP-02` Jei informacija gali paveikti kolegų darbą, pasidalinu ja nelaukdamas, kol jie paklaus.
- `COP-03` Kai sprendimui trūksta žinių, kreipiuosi į žmogų, kuris jas turi.
- `COP-05` Jei užduotį galiu atlikti savarankiškai, dažniausiai taip ir darau, o ne derinu su kitais.

### Construct signal

The cognitive pilot already exposed that the reverse item is not `low cooperation`; it expresses a legitimate autonomy preference.

This provides unusually direct evidence for reframing the construct as:

**Autonomous execution ↔ Collaborative coordination**

The behavioural space must be held constant: how a person tends to organise work / decisions when either independent or coordinated execution is realistically possible.

Both poles can be useful:
- autonomy reduces coordination cost and can accelerate ownership;
- collaboration increases shared information, alignment and joint problem-solving.

### Item-bank warning

`sharing information` and `asking an expert` are not automatically the same construct as collaboration preference. They can be rational necessities.

Future items must present situations where both independent and collaborative routes are viable rather than testing whether the respondent knows that information should be shared.

### Boundary with Social Energy

Keep distinct:
- Cooperation = how much work/decision-making is coordinated with others;
- Social Energy = how visibly/actively one participates in group interaction.

A reserved person may collaborate extensively; a socially energetic person may still prefer individual ownership.

### Boundary with future leadership competence

Autonomy preference must not be interpreted as poor delegation or low teamwork competence. WorkStyle measures preference/default; competence layers remain separate.

### Decision

**KEEP as a strong bipolar core axis.**

Working pole pair:

`Autonomous execution ↔ Collaborative coordination`

Status: STRONG BIPOLAR.

---

## 3. Result

Both constructs survive as anchors of the reduced model.

| Construct | Working axis | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Work Structure | Structured preparation ↔ Emergent organisation | STRONG BIPOLAR |
| Cooperation | Autonomous execution ↔ Collaborative coordination | STRONG BIPOLAR |

They should be among the first constructs used in Stage 0 pole and item-form cognitive work because their two ends can be described without a hidden `good` direction.
