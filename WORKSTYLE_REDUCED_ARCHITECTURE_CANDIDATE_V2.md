# WorkStyle — Reduced Architecture Candidate V2

Status: CONSOLIDATED CONSTRUCT HYPOTHESIS / NOT VALIDATED / PRE-ITEM-WRITING
Date: 2026-08-23
Branch: hero-webgl

## 1. Change from V1

The previous reduced architecture proposed 9 directional axes.

Respondent-level review identified that four axes could feel semantically/emotionally similar because each could be heard as a version of:

> `hold my course ↔ change / adapt / accommodate`

The overlap cluster was:
- Method Consistency ↔ Method Adaptation;
- Commitment Stability ↔ Goal Reallocation;
- Position Stability ↔ Position Revisability;
- Position Assertion ↔ Accommodation.

The issue is not only latent construct overlap. Even theoretically distinct constructs can collapse in the respondent's mind if their language, emotional tone and response logic are too similar.

Decision:
- retain Method Consistency ↔ Method Adaptation;
- remove Commitment Stability ↔ Goal Reallocation from V1;
- retain the cognitive revision construct but rename/reframe it around **conclusions and evidence**, not `position`;
- retain the interpersonal assertion construct but rename/reframe it around **disagreement behaviour**, not `position`.

Result: **8-axis directional candidate core**.

# 2. Recommended 8-axis candidate core

## A. Work execution

### A1. Structured Preparation ↔ Emergent Organisation

Working LT concept:
`Struktūruotas pasiruošimas ↔ Organizavimas eigoje`

Measurement question:
> Do I usually create structure before acting, or let structure emerge while work is already moving?

Referent: **work organisation before / during execution**.

Status: STRONG BIPOLAR CANDIDATE.

---

### A2. Method Consistency ↔ Method Adaptation

Working LT concept:
`Metodo nuoseklumas ↔ Metodo adaptavimas`

Measurement question:
> Once a workable method is in use, how readily do I preserve it versus modify it as feedback and conditions evolve?

Referent: **the method used to pursue an already broadly stable objective**.

Status: STRONG BIPOLAR CANDIDATE.

Important distinction:
This axis is not about changing the objective itself.

---

## B. Cognitive framing

### B1. Local / Concrete Focus ↔ System / Relational Focus

Working LT concept:
`Konkretus fokusas ↔ Ryšių ir sistemos fokusas`

Measurement question:
> When an issue appears, do I tend first to focus on the immediate concrete part or scan dependencies and wider effects?

Referent: **default level of analysis**.

Status: PROMISING / NEEDS CAREFUL COGNITIVE TESTING.

Boundary:
This is not a systems-thinking ability score.

---

### B2. Conclusion Stability ↔ Evidence-led Revision

Replaces previous wording `Position Stability ↔ Position Revisability`.

Working LT concept:
`Išvados stabilumas ↔ Išvados peržiūrėjimas pagal naujus duomenis`

Measurement question:
> After I have formed a conclusion, how readily do new facts or credible counter-evidence lead me to reopen it?

Referent: **updating a conclusion in response to evidence**.

Status: CONDITIONAL BIPOLAR CANDIDATE.

Important distinction:
This is not about what I do when another person disagrees with me.

Design rule:
Future items should contain **new evidence / information**, not interpersonal disagreement alone.

---

### B3. Opportunity Scanning ↔ Risk Scanning

Working LT concept:
`Galimybių skenavimas ↔ Rizikų skenavimas`

Measurement question:
> When considering a new option or uncertain situation, what tends to receive my attention first: possible upside or possible failure / risk conditions?

Referent: **initial attentional orientation under uncertainty**.

Status: CONDITIONAL BIPOLAR CANDIDATE.

Boundary:
This is not optimism ↔ pessimism.

---

## C. Interpersonal style

### C1. Autonomous Execution ↔ Collaborative Coordination

Working LT concept:
`Savarankiškas veikimas ↔ Koordinavimas su kitais`

Measurement question:
> When both routes are viable, do I tend to proceed independently or coordinate work / decisions with others?

Referent: **coordination of work with other people**.

Status: STRONG BIPOLAR CANDIDATE.

Boundary:
Autonomy is not poor teamwork; coordination is not dependency.

---

### C2. Direct Disagreement ↔ Alignment-seeking

Replaces previous wording `Position Assertion ↔ Accommodation`.

Working LT concept:
`Tiesus nesutarimo išsakymas ↔ Derinimosi paieška`

Measurement question:
> When I disagree with someone, do I tend to put the disagreement explicitly on the table or first work toward alignment / reduced friction?

Referent: **interpersonal handling of disagreement**.

Status: STRONG BIPOLAR CANDIDATE.

Important distinction:
This is not about revising my conclusion because new evidence appears.

Design rule:
Future items should contain **interpersonal disagreement / competing preferences**, not simply new information.

---

### C3. Expressive Participation ↔ Reserved Participation

Working LT concept:
`Aktyvus dalyvavimas ↔ Santūrus dalyvavimas`

Measurement question:
> How visibly and actively do I tend to occupy space in group interaction?

Referent: **social/group participation intensity**.

Status: STRONG BIPOLAR CANDIDATE.

Boundary:
This is not assertiveness and not collaboration.

---

# 3. Removed from V1 after respondent-similarity challenge

## Commitment Stability ↔ Goal Reallocation

Status: PARKED / NOT IN 8-AXIS V1.

Reason:
- theoretically distinguishable from method adaptation;
- but highly context-dependent on goal value, authority, sunk cost, alternatives and new information;
- likely to attract `depends on situation` responses;
- creates another `hold ↔ change` emotional grammar already represented elsewhere;
- risks drifting from stable WorkStyle toward judgement quality.

It may return only if later qualitative evidence demonstrates a recognisable recurring personal tendency that is distinct from Method Adaptation and Achievement Drive.

# 5. Respondent-distinctness rule

From V2 onward, construct separation must pass two different tests:

## A. Latent distinctness
Are the constructs theoretically and empirically different?

## B. Felt distinctness
Does a respondent experience the questions as asking about different parts of work life?

A pair fails V1 if respondents consistently paraphrase both as the same personal dilemma even if a theorist can distinguish them.

This is now a formal design rule.

---

# 6. Distinctness anchors for the four formerly similar axes

Only three remain in V2.

### Method Consistency ↔ Method Adaptation
Trigger: **feedback / changed conditions during execution**.
Object being changed: **method**.
Social other required? No.
Evidence required? Not necessarily.

### Conclusion Stability ↔ Evidence-led Revision
Trigger: **new facts / counter-evidence**.
Object being changed: **conclusion / interpretation**.
Social other required? No.
Method change required? No.

### Direct Disagreement ↔ Alignment-seeking
Trigger: **another person's differing preference / position**.
Object being managed: **interaction / disagreement**.
Conclusion change required? No.
Method change required? No.

If an item cannot be cleanly assigned to exactly one of these referents, it should be rejected or rewritten.

---

# 7. Measurement grammar

Research default remains:
- 5-position semantic differential;
- both anchors explicit;
- separate `Priklauso nuo situacijos`;
- separate `Neteko tokios situacijos / negaliu įvertinti`;
- signed coding only as internal research convenience;
- ordinal modelling;
- no 0–100 profile;
- no numerical domain averages;
- no outward-is-better radar.

Development pool target remains 8–12 candidate situations/items per axis before reduction.

---

# 8. Parked constructs

Not part of primary 8-axis V1:
- Achievement Drive — optional unipolar;
- Commitment Stability / Goal Reallocation — context-heavy candidate;
- Persuasive Influence — competence contaminated / overlaps agency;
- Learning Orientation — heterogeneous;
- Empathic Attention — no clean opposite;
- Emotional Regulation — self-regulation capability / temperament;
- Reflective Self-Awareness — metacognitive / no clean opposite;
- Pace/Tempo — research candidate only;
- Conflict Engagement — currently context within Direct Disagreement;
- Detail/Gist — monitor within analysis-level axis.

---

# 9. Product boundary

The 8-axis candidate remains a WorkStyle reflection core:

> **How do I usually tend to approach work when more than one legitimate way of acting exists?**

It does not measure:
- competence;
- intelligence;
- leadership potential;
- role fit;
- performance;
- DISC;
- PAEI.

Leadership SJT remains parked as a separate future professional module.

---

# 10. Next methodological step

Before generating a large item bank, perform a Stage 0 pole-definition and felt-distinctness audit for all eight axes.

For each axis define:
1. same behavioural space;
2. legitimate value of each pole;
3. common trigger/context;
4. what changes / receives attention;
5. nearest neighbouring axis and explicit exclusion rule;
6. situations where both ends can be sensible;
7. likely social-desirability bias;
8. likely `depends on context` failure mode.

Only axes that survive this gate move to candidate item generation.
