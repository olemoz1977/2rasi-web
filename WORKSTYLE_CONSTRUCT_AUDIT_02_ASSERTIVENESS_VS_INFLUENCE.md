# WorkStyle construct audit 02 — Assertive Communication vs Persuasive Influence

Status: STAGE 0 / CONSTRUCT AUDIT / PRE-ITEM-WRITING
Date: 2026-08-23

## Purpose

Resolve whether current WorkStyle constructs:
- `Assertive Communication / Asertyvus komunikavimas` (AST), and
- `Persuasive Influence / Įtaka` (INF)

represent two separable work-style dimensions or whether the current architecture is splitting one broader interpersonal / agentic tendency into two weak scales.

No new item bank is authorised by this audit.

---

## 1. What the current v0.6 items actually ask

### AST — Assertive Communication

Current items:
- AST-01: state disagreement clearly and respectfully;
- AST-02: state when extra work would interfere with a more important priority and negotiate priorities;
- AST-05R: yield on a low-importance disagreement to avoid tension.

The behavioural referent is primarily:

> **how readily a person makes and maintains their position visible when interests, priorities or opinions differ.**

This is a plausible work-style preference.

The current reverse item already suggests a meaningful alternative pole:

`position assertion  ↔  accommodation / harmony preservation`

Neither pole must be framed as globally better.

### INF — Persuasive Influence

Current items:
- INF-01: explain how an idea benefits people one wants to involve;
- INF-02: explain an idea another way when people do not initially understand it;
- INF-05R: stop pushing an idea when others disagree.

The item set mixes at least three different things:
- adapting explanation to the audience;
- communication skill / clarity;
- persistence in advocating an idea.

This is not yet a clean style construct.

INF-01 and INF-02 are especially vulnerable to becoming self-rated **competence**:
`I explain persuasively / I can find another way to explain.`

INF-05 overlaps strongly with AST's question of whether one continues to hold / advocate a position when there is interpersonal resistance.

---

## 2. Theoretical check: interpersonal agency

Interpersonal Circumplex traditions organise interpersonal functioning primarily around Agency and Communion.

Agency includes behaviours and motives related to:
- assertion;
- dominance / influence;
- autonomy;
- control or impact on others.

Therefore assertiveness and interpersonal influence are expected to be related expressions of an agentic direction rather than automatically separate person-level dimensions.

This does not prove they are identical.

A useful conceptual distinction can exist between:
- **making one's own position visible / maintaining it** (assertiveness), and
- **actively attempting to change another person's position** (persuasion).

However, the key WorkStyle question is not whether these behaviours are semantically distinct. It is whether a short self-report instrument can measure them as stable, discriminable work-style tendencies without drifting into communication competence.

Current v0.6 content does not demonstrate that distinction.

---

## 3. Competence contamination in INF

A WorkStyle scale should primarily measure a person's usual behavioural orientation, not whether they believe they are effective at a socially valued skill.

Examples of contamination:

`If people do not understand my idea, I explain it another way.`

This can mean:
- cognitive flexibility;
- communication skill;
- patience;
- motivation to persuade;
- audience awareness.

It is not specific to a stable `influence style`.

Likewise:

`I explain how the idea is useful to people I want to involve.`

is close to a recommended persuasion technique. Endorsing it may reflect knowledge of effective communication rather than a spontaneous preference.

Therefore `Persuasive Influence` in its current form should not survive merely because influence matters at work.

---

## 4. Merge / retain options considered

### Option A — Keep AST and INF as separate dimensions

Not supported conceptually at this stage.

Would require evidence that:
- respondents understand the two differently;
- item sets show discriminant structure;
- INF can be written without ability / effectiveness claims;
- AST and INF do not simply become two highly correlated expressions of Agency.

### Option B — Merge both into one broad Agency scale

Too broad.

It would risk mixing:
- assertiveness;
- dominance;
- persuasion;
- social energy;
- autonomy.

The existing WorkStyle architecture already has Social Energy and Cooperation/Autonomy candidates, so a broad Agency score would create new overlap.

### Option C — Retain AST as a bipolar style axis; remove INF as a separate scored construct

**Current preferred hypothesis.**

AST can be refined as a neutral interpersonal trade-off:

`Position advocacy / assertion  ↔  Accommodation / harmony preservation`

Persuasion / influence can remain:
- a behavioural context used in AST items;
- a future competence / SJT / interview construct if the professional assessment layer needs it;
- a possible external interpretation signal, but not a WorkStyle score by default.

## 5. Revised AST candidate

### Working name

Not final public wording.

Candidate conceptual label:

**Position Assertion ↔ Accommodation**

Possible Lithuanian concept language:

`Pozicijos gynimas ↔ Prisitaikantis derinimas`

or

`Pozicijos išsakymas ↔ Santykio / harmonijos išsaugojimas`

Public wording requires later cognitive testing.

### Working definition

> The person's usual interpersonal threshold for explicitly stating and maintaining a position versus accommodating others or reducing friction when opinions, priorities or interests differ.

### Bipolarity status

**STRONG BIPOLAR CANDIDATE.**

Both poles can be functional:
- stronger assertion supports clarity, boundary-setting and visible disagreement;
- stronger accommodation supports relationship preservation, consensus and selective yielding.

Failure modes exist at both extremes, but the scale must measure preference, not dysfunction.

### Critical design constraints

Do not contrast:
- `courageous` with `weak`;
- `honest` with `conflict avoidant`;
- `clear` with `passive`.

The accommodation pole must be written as a legitimate strategic choice, not as lack of backbone.

Items should vary:
- importance of the disagreement;
- relationship stakes;
- time pressure;
- decision ownership.

But item context must not make one response obviously correct.

---

## 6. What happens to Persuasive Influence

### Current decision

**DROP INF AS A SEPARATE WORKSTYLE CONSTRUCT — provisional Stage 0 decision.**

Reason:
- current items are competence-contaminated;
- the behavioural persistence component overlaps AST;
- persuasion as effectiveness is not a neutral preference axis;
- a legitimate opposite pole is unclear (`non-directive` easily becomes passivity).

This does **not** mean influence is unimportant.

Influence may belong elsewhere:
- Leadership / managerial competence assessment;
- structured interview;
- situational judgement;
- future interpersonal behaviour research;
- empirical correspondence to broader Agency / DISC-like space.

WorkStyle should not keep a construct merely because it is important to managers.

---

## 7. Relationship to Social Energy

Dropping INF does not make Social Energy redundant.

Potential distinction:
- Social Energy = how visibly / actively a person tends to enter group interaction;
- Position Assertion = what they do when a position, boundary or disagreement must be expressed.

A quiet / reserved person can still be highly assertive when required.
A socially energetic person can still accommodate disagreements.

This separation is conceptually coherent and should be tested empirically.

---

## 8. Relationship to Cooperation ↔ Autonomy

Cooperation / Autonomy concerns **how work and decisions are coordinated with others**.

Position Assertion / Accommodation concerns **how differences in position are handled interpersonally**.

Expected correlation is acceptable; they should not be assumed identical.

---

## 9. Architecture impact

This is the first construct audit that supports a real reduction in dimension count.

Previous audit:
- PER + ADP merge rejected;
- two cleaner hypotheses remain.

This audit:
- AST survives in redesigned bipolar form;
- INF is removed as a separate scored WorkStyle candidate.

If no later evidence reinstates INF, the inherited 15-construct architecture moves toward **14 candidates before other merges / drops**.

The final count remains open and must not be targeted for visual symmetry.

---

## 10. Current decision

**AST — RETAIN / REDESIGN as strong bipolar candidate.**

**INF — DROP as separate WorkStyle score, unless later empirical or cognitive evidence establishes a genuinely distinct non-ability style construct.**

Do not write final items yet.

Recommended next construct audit:

`Systems Thinking vs Learning Orientation`

Reason:
Both methodology reviews raised overlap / analytical-exploration concerns, and the current v0.6 items mix causal analysis, learning from mistakes, seeking dissenting views and method change.
