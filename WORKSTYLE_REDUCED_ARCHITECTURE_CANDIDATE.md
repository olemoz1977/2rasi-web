# WorkStyle — Reduced Architecture Candidate

Status: CONSOLIDATED CONSTRUCT HYPOTHESIS / NOT VALIDATED / PRE-ITEM-WRITING
Date: 2026-08-23
Branch: hero-webgl

## 1. Why this document exists

The inherited WorkStyle architecture contained 15 constructs grouped into five domains.

After:
- v0.5/v0.6 cognitive evidence;
- the bipolar methodology hypothesis;
- two independent methodological reviews;
- ten construct/redundancy/gap audits;

the number 15 is no longer treated as a target.

This document consolidates the strongest remaining work-style hypotheses without preserving old dimensions for symmetry.

It does **not** authorise a new item bank yet.

---

# 2. Recommended next core: 9 directional axes

The cleanest next research architecture is a **9-axis bipolar candidate core**.

This is a deliberate simplification from the earlier hybrid 15-construct design.

Why a primarily bipolar core is now possible:
- constructs that had no legitimate opposite were not forced into bipolar form;
- instead, several were dropped, parked, or moved to other layers;
- the remaining core constructs all describe a recurring work-style choice, allocation, or attentional direction with potentially legitimate value at both ends.

Important:
**semantic bipolarity is still only a hypothesis.** Every axis must later demonstrate that respondents experience the poles as one coherent behavioural space.

---

# 3. Working descriptive families

For navigation and explanation only, the nine axes can be organised into three descriptive families.

These families are **not numerical domains** and are not yet higher-order factors.

## A. Execution orientation

### A1. Structured Preparation ↔ Emergent Organisation

Source: old `Work Structure / ORG`.

Question:
> How much do I tend to structure work in advance versus organise it while acting?

Status: **STRONG BIPOLAR**.

Both poles can be functional:
- structure → predictability, coordination, repeatability;
- emergence / improvisation → speed, flexibility, low overhead.

---

### A2. Method Consistency ↔ Method Adaptation

Source: redesigned `Adaptability / ADP`, absorbing the useful method-related part of old LRN.

Question:
> Once a work method is in use, how readily do I preserve versus modify it as conditions and feedback evolve?

Status: **STRONG BIPOLAR**.

Both poles can be functional:
- consistency → stability, standardisation, accumulated learning;
- adaptation → responsiveness, experimentation, contextual fit.

---

### A3. Commitment Stability ↔ Goal Reallocation

Source: redesigned `Goal Persistence / PER`.

Question:
> Once a goal or priority is chosen, how high is my threshold for maintaining versus reallocating commitment when progress is difficult or new competing information appears?

Status: **CONDITIONAL BIPOLAR**.

Critical gate:
If respondents experience this almost entirely as `it depends on the importance of the goal`, it should be dropped rather than scored.

---

## B. Cognitive framing

### B1. Local / Concrete Focus ↔ System / Relational Focus

Source: redesigned `Systems Thinking / SYS`.

Question:
> At what level do I tend to frame a work issue first: immediate/local or broader/dependency-based?

Status: **PROMISING BIPOLAR / NEEDS CAREFUL COGNITIVE TESTING**.

Boundary:
This must measure default framing, not ability to think systemically.

---

### B2. Position Stability ↔ Position Revisability

Source: redesigned `Intellectual Humility / HUM`.

Latent label: **Position Revision Threshold**.

Question:
> How much counter-evidence or alternative perspective is typically needed before I revise a settled conclusion?

Status: **CONDITIONAL BIPOLAR**.

Boundary:
Do not frame the poles as `open-minded ↔ stubborn` or `humble ↔ arrogant`.

---

### B3. Opportunity Scanning ↔ Risk Scanning

Source: redesigned `Constructive Outlook / OUT`.

Question:
> When evaluating a proposal, uncertain situation, or possible change, what do I tend to notice / examine first: potential possibilities or possible failure / risk conditions?

Status: **CONDITIONAL BIPOLAR**.

Boundary:
This is not optimism ↔ pessimism.

---

## C. Interpersonal style

### C1. Autonomous Execution ↔ Collaborative Coordination

Source: redesigned `Cooperation / COP`.

Question:
> When both independent and coordinated routes are viable, how much do I tend to work / decide autonomously versus coordinate with others?

Status: **STRONG BIPOLAR**.

Boundary:
Autonomy is not poor teamwork; collaboration is not dependency.

---

### C2. Position Assertion ↔ Accommodation

Source: redesigned `Assertive Communication / AST`, absorbing the style-relevant portion of `Persuasive Influence / INF`.

Question:
> When opinions, priorities, or interests differ, how readily do I make / maintain my position versus accommodate to preserve alignment or reduce friction?

Status: **STRONG BIPOLAR**.

Boundary:
Accommodation must remain a legitimate pole, not a euphemism for passivity.

---

### C3. Expressive Participation ↔ Reserved Participation

Source: `Social Energy / ENG`.

Question:
> How visibly and actively do I tend to occupy social/group interaction space?

Status: **STRONG BIPOLAR**.

Boundary:
Social energy is not assertiveness and not collaboration.

# 5. Constructs removed from the primary core

## Dropped as inherited constructs

### Persuasive Influence / INF
Reason:
- competence contamination;
- overlap with assertion / agency;
- no legitimate clear opposite pole.

### Learning Orientation / LRN
Reason:
- heterogeneous item content;
- useful fragments reassigned to Method Adaptation and Position Revision;
- Explore/Exploit dropped as separate axis because of redundancy.

### Empathic Attention / EMP
Reason:
- empathy and factual attention can both be high;
- no neutral opposite;
- strong desirability / competence contamination.

### Emotional Regulation / REG
Reason:
- mixes deliberation, criticism response and recovery speed;
- regulation is not the opposite of expression;
- stronger fit to temperament / self-regulation capability than neutral WorkStyle.

### Reflective Self-Awareness / REF
Reason:
- no legitimate opposite;
- metacognitive / developmental rather than neutral style;
- self-report circularity risk.

# 6. Parked / optional constructs

## Achievement Drive / ACH

Status: **OPTIONAL UNIPOLAR / PARKED**.

It remains conceptually relevant but is not included in the primary 9-axis directional core because:
- it has no clean opposite;
- `more` is socially desirable;
- it would introduce a second scoring grammar into V1;
- compact directional WorkStyle should first be tested without it.

It can be reintroduced later if the product deliberately chooses a broader hybrid profile.

## Pace / Tempo

Status: NEW RESEARCH CANDIDATE / PARKED.

Potential axis:
`Deliberative pace ↔ Rapid action pace`.

Not added now because context and risk level may dominate the response.

## Conflict Engagement

Not added separately. Use as item context for Position Assertion ↔ Accommodation unless evidence supports separation.

## Detail ↔ Gist

Not added separately. Monitor inside Local ↔ System framing until evidence supports separation.

# 7. What happens to the old five domains

Old public domains:
- Įgyvendinimas;
- Adaptacija;
- Atvirumas kitiems;
- Poveikis;
- Savireguliacija.

They are now **historical architecture**, not the target numerical model.

Do not:
- preserve five domain scores by averaging new axes;
- force the new axes back into 5×3 symmetry;
- treat the old domains as validated latent factors.

The new 3-family grouping is descriptive only and may change after item/data work.

# 8. Measurement grammar for the 9-axis candidate

Preferred research grammar:

- 5-position two-anchor semantic differential;
- both poles visible and equally legitimate;
- separate `Priklauso nuo situacijos` response;
- separate `Neteko tokios situacijos / negaliu įvertinti` response;
- internal signed coding for research convenience only;
- ordinal modelling;
- no 0–100 result;
- no outward-is-better radar;
- no numerical family/domain averages.

Important:
The next item bank must not start at 3 items per axis.

Development pool target:
**8–12 candidate items/scenarios per axis**, then reduce after cognitive and empirical evidence.

---

# 9. Stage 0 gate for each axis

Before item-bank generation, every axis must pass four tests.

## 1. Same behavioural space
Both poles must answer the same underlying work-choice question.

## 2. Simultaneous-high test
If a person can naturally be strongly high on both poles in the same referent, the proposed axis is probably artificial.

## 3. Legitimacy test
Experienced workers should be able to describe realistic conditions where either pole is useful.

## 4. Competence-contamination test
The item must not become `do I know what the objectively good worker should do?`

Conditional axes A3, B1, B2 and B3 require the strongest challenge.

---

# 10. Product interpretation boundary

The 9-axis core measures **usual work-style direction**, not:
- competence;
- leadership potential;
- role fit;
- performance;
- DISC;
- PAEI.

PAEI and DISC remain optional future interpretation lenses.

Leadership SJT remains a separate future professional module and is currently parked from the WorkStyle development path.

# 11. Strategic fork before item writing

The construct-methodology work now reaches a product-level decision that should not be made silently.

## Option A — Clean directional core (recommended research default)

V1 tests only the 9 bipolar candidate axes above.

Advantages:
- one response grammar;
- one visual grammar;
- strongly non-evaluative philosophy;
- simpler cognitive testing;
- avoids mixing `how much` traits with `which direction` axes.

Trade-off:
- intentionally leaves out useful but unipolar constructs such as Achievement Drive.

## Option B — Broader hybrid profile

V1 includes the 9 directional axes plus one or more unipolar scales, beginning with Achievement Drive.

Advantages:
- broader description of work behaviour;
- closer continuity with historical WorkStyle 15.

Trade-offs:
- two measurement grammars;
- harder result design;
- stronger `high = better` contamination;
- more respondent burden;
- more complex validation.

### Recommended methodological default

**Option A first.**

A clean 9-axis research core can later add an optional unipolar layer. The reverse transition is harder because mixed scoring becomes embedded in UI, interpretation and user expectations.

This fork is strategic/product-level, not merely technical.

No new item bank should be written until the fork is explicitly chosen.
