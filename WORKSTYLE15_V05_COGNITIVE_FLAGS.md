## Strongest structural signal

Two dimensions need a full rewrite rather than sentence-level polishing:

### Adaptability / Prisitaikymas
All three v0.5 items were flagged:
- ADP-01
- ADP-04
- ADP-05

Problem pattern: the items are too dependent on whether the situation *actually requires* a change. They risk measuring whether the respondent thinks change is justified rather than their tendency to adapt.

### Constructive Outlook / Konstruktyvus požiūris
All three v0.5 items were flagged:
- OUT-02
- OUT-03
- OUT-05

Problem pattern: wording is abstract (`galima baigtis`, `realus žingsnis`, `užstringu`) and some reverse wording creates an obvious desirable/undesirable answer.

## Other dimensions needing targeted rewrite

### Goal Persistence / Kryptingumas
Flagged 2 of 3:
- PER-04 — NA
- PER-05

Need clearer separation between persistence over time, persistence after setbacks, and rational switching.

### Cooperation / Bendradarbiavimas
Flagged 2 of 3:
- COP-02 — NA
- COP-03

Need more observable behaviours and less dependence on vague qualifiers such as `svarbi informacija` or `išmano geriau`.

### Reflective Self-Awareness / Reflektyvi savimonė
Flagged 2 of 3:
- REF-01
- REF-05 — NA

Current items ask respondents to judge hidden internal processes or make morally loaded self-attributions. Rewrite toward observable post-event reflection behaviours.

## Plain-language rewrite principles confirmed by the session

1. One read must be enough.
2. Prefer observable behaviour over self-analysis.
3. Avoid asking whether a person is `good` or `bad` at something by implication.
4. Avoid forced either/or formulations when both can be true depending on context.
5. Avoid vague nouns such as `situacija`, `rezultatas`, `problema`, `svarbi informacija` unless the behaviour is still unambiguous.
6. Reverse items should describe a legitimate opposite work style, not an obviously undesirable behaviour.
7. `Sunku įvertinti` remains separate from midpoint `3`; never recode it as 3.
8. Do not patch a dimension item-by-item when 2/3 or 3/3 items show friction; rebuild the trio from the construct definition.
