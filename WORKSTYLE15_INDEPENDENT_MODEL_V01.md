# WorkStyle 15 — independent construct model v0.1

Status: WORKING MODEL / PRE-PILOT
Last updated: 2026-08-23

## Why this model exists

The legacy 15-dimension / 6-style structure closely matches the published ADEPT-15 architecture. The British Psychological Society summary states that ADEPT-15 contains 15 traits grouped into 6 higher-order styles, with 10 traits mapped to the Five Factor Model and 5 additional workplace/leadership-related traits.

WorkStyle 15 should therefore not present the inherited 6-style arrangement as an independently developed model. The preferred direction is to keep the useful 15-work-tendency idea while reorganizing the constructs into a transparent, original workplace-reflection architecture grounded in broader personality/work-behaviour concepts.

This is not a claim of psychometric validation. It is a construct design for pilot testing.

## Proposed architecture: 5 domains × 3 dimensions

### 1. Execution
How a person turns intent into sustained, organized action.

1. **Goal Persistence / Kryptingumas**
   - maintaining focus on important goals;
   - continuing through obstacles;
   - resisting premature switching.
   - conceptual anchor: conscientiousness / diligence / persistence.

2. **Work Structure / Struktūra**
   - planning;
   - organizing information and tasks;
   - creating repeatable ways of working.
   - conceptual anchor: conscientiousness / organization.

3. **Achievement Drive / Pasiekimų siekis**
   - choosing stretching goals;
   - wanting greater responsibility or impact;
   - continuing beyond merely acceptable performance.
   - conceptual anchor: achievement motivation / striving.

### 2. Adaptation
How a person understands change, updates assumptions and learns.

4. **Systems Thinking / Sisteminis mąstymas**
   - seeing patterns and relationships;
   - thinking beyond the immediate case;
   - considering longer-term consequences.
   - conceptual anchor: openness/intellect and conceptual reasoning.

5. **Adaptability / Prisitaikymas**
   - revising approach when evidence changes;
   - working productively under changing conditions;
   - distinguishing what must stay stable from what can change.
   - conceptual anchor: openness/flexibility and adaptive behaviour.

6. **Learning Orientation / Mokymosi orientacija**
   - seeking feedback;
   - reflecting after action;
   - turning mistakes and experience into changed behaviour.
   - conceptual anchor: learning goal orientation / mastery orientation.

### 3. Collaboration
How a person makes room for other people in joint work.

7. **Cooperation / Bendradarbiavimas**
   - sharing information;
   - involving relevant expertise;
   - coordinating rather than defaulting to solo action.
   - conceptual anchor: agreeableness / cooperation.

8. **Empathic Attention / Empatinis dėmesys**
   - noticing emotional and interpersonal signals;
   - considering how decisions affect people;
   - checking interpretations rather than assuming them.
   - conceptual anchor: agreeableness / empathy / interpersonal sensitivity.

9. **Intellectual Humility / Intelektinis nuolankumas**
   - acknowledging error;
   - changing position when evidence is stronger;
   - recognizing others' contribution;
   - asking for help without status defence.
   - conceptual anchor: humility / modesty / openness to correction.

### 4. Influence
How a person expresses position, mobilizes others and contributes social energy.

10. **Assertive Communication / Asertyvus komunikavimas**
    - stating disagreement clearly and respectfully;
    - setting boundaries;
    - addressing difficult issues directly.
    - conceptual anchor: extraversion/assertiveness and interpersonal agency.

11. **Persuasive Influence / Įtaka**
    - framing ideas for different audiences;
    - building support without relying on formal authority;
    - helping groups move toward a direction.
    - conceptual anchor: social influence / leadership emergence / interpersonal impact.

12. **Social Energy / Socialinė energija**
    - visible engagement;
    - initiating movement when group energy drops;
    - bringing enthusiasm into interaction.
    - conceptual anchor: extraversion / liveliness / activation.

### 5. Self-Regulation
How a person manages internal reactions and learns from their own impact.

13. **Emotional Regulation / Emocinė savitvarda**
    - remaining functional under pressure;
    - separating facts from first reaction;
    - recovering working rhythm after disruption.
    - conceptual anchor: emotional stability / regulation.

14. **Constructive Outlook / Konstruktyvus požiūris**
    - seeing possible action under difficulty;
    - maintaining realistic hope;
    - recovering willingness to try again after setbacks.
    - conceptual anchor: optimism / resilience / positive affect, without assuming high positivity is always better.

15. **Reflective Self-Awareness / Reflektyvi savimonė**
    - noticing when one's usual approach stops working;
    - distinguishing intention from impact;
    - recognizing one's own contribution to a difficult interaction.
    - conceptual anchor: metacognition / self-awareness / reflective practice.

## What changes from the inherited architecture

- Drop the inherited 6-style grouping as the primary WorkStyle model.
- Use 5 original workplace domains: Execution, Adaptation, Collaboration, Influence, Self-Regulation.
- Move Ambition/Achievement Drive into Execution rather than keeping a separate Achievement style.
- Separate Persuasive Influence from Assertive Communication instead of treating both as a generic power/interaction construct.
- Rename Humility toward Intellectual Humility to emphasize openness to correction rather than submissiveness.
- Rename Positivity toward Constructive Outlook to avoid implying that positivity is inherently good.
- Rename Liveliness toward Social Energy to make the workplace meaning clearer.
- Rename Composure toward Emotional Regulation to emphasize functioning under pressure rather than emotional suppression.
- Rename Conceptual Thinking toward Systems Thinking to move closer to observable workplace reasoning.
- Keep 15 dimensions because the breadth is useful, not because ADEPT-15 uses 15.

## Relationship to broader frameworks

This model is not a Big Five or HEXACO test.

The broad personality frameworks are used only as conceptual anchors:
- conscientiousness helps anchor persistence and structure;
- openness/intellect helps anchor systems thinking and adaptability;
- agreeableness helps anchor cooperation and empathic attention;
- extraversion helps anchor assertive communication and social energy;
- emotional stability helps anchor emotional regulation;
- HEXACO humility/modesty literature helps distinguish humility-related behaviour from agreeableness.

Several WorkStyle dimensions intentionally remain workplace-specific rather than being forced into Big Five categories, especially Learning Orientation, Persuasive Influence, Achievement Drive and Reflective Self-Awareness.

## PAEI / DISC lenses after this change

PAEI and DISC remain optional derived lenses, but their mapping must now be rebuilt against these 15 revised constructs rather than copied from the legacy architecture.

Likely conceptual directions for later testing:
- P: Goal Persistence, Achievement Drive, Assertive Communication;
- A: Work Structure, Goal Persistence;
- E: Systems Thinking, Adaptability, Learning Orientation, Achievement Drive;
- I: Cooperation, Empathic Attention, Intellectual Humility, Reflective Self-Awareness.

DISC likely directions:
- D: Assertive Communication, Achievement Drive, Goal Persistence, Persuasive Influence;
- I: Social Energy, Persuasive Influence, Constructive Outlook;
- S: Cooperation, Empathic Attention, Emotional Regulation;
- C: Work Structure, Goal Persistence, Systems Thinking.

These are hypotheses only and should not be scored numerically before item-level review and pilot evidence.

## Immediate implication for the 75-item bank

The existing original 75 LT statements should not be discarded. Most can be remapped into this 5×3 architecture with wording changes limited to constructs that changed meaning.

Highest-priority wording review areas:
- old Ambicija -> Achievement Drive;
- old Nuolankumas -> Intellectual Humility;
- old Konceptualus mąstymas -> Systems Thinking;
- old Ramybė -> Emotional Regulation;
- old Pozityvumas -> Constructive Outlook;
- old Gyvybingumas -> Social Energy;
- old Įtaka -> Persuasive Influence.

Only after this remapping should the LT beta wording be frozen and coded.

## Validation boundary

WorkStyle 15 v0.1 is a reflection model under development, not a validated personality assessment.

Public beta language should therefore avoid:
- diagnostic claims;
- population percentiles without norms;
- claims that the model is equivalent to ADEPT-15, Big Five, HEXACO, DISC or PAEI;
- fixed type labels.

The goal is useful self-observation at work, followed by empirical refinement from pilot data.
